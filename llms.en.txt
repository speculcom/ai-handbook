# AI Handbook (ai-handbook)

> Learn AI · use AI tools · run AI models — one handbook, three parts.

One handbook, three parts: `terms/` (Learn AI — terminology map, learning paths, guides) · `tools/` (AI tools — product profiles compared on fixed dimensions) · `models/` (AI models — quantization index, lineage cards). All three share the brand and shell under `shared/` (exactly one copy). Every judgment carries an official source; unknown is written as unknown; no overall ranking.

## Size (computed from data — see the build scripts under `scripts/` and `_audit/gen-repo-docs.mjs`)

One repo, three parts — 200 terms arranged into six stations by "what to learn first" (Getting In / Hands On / Going Deeper / Under the Hood / Running It / Choosing Right); 8 curated learning paths with checkpoints; 55 short guides; one chronicle-and-atlas page (18 timeline entries, 12 companies, 5 vocabulary shifts); 470 source entries; English coverage 200/200. 177 profiles across 10 categories (coding 41, chat 14, image 13, video 12, audio 10, research 11, automation 14, framework 22, mcp 28, runner 12), compared across a fixed set of 8 dimensions. 11 series / 16 model repos / 132 quant variants / 1253 GGUF files, from 55 quantizers.

## Content map

- `terms/` — Learn AI: terminology map (six stations), learning paths, short guides, chronicle & atlas
- `tools/` — AI tools: 10 categories (coding / chat / image / video / audio / research / automation / framework / mcp / runner) × eight-dimension profiles
- `models/` — AI models: quantization index, family lineage cards, closed-model contrast cards, model-making guides
- `shared/brand/` — Site-wide shared brand assets (the single vendored copy)

## What this repo does not do

- No overall ranking — a weighted total hides trade-offs
- No hands-on testing (without lab data, write "unknown")
- No hosting of any model weights or third-party binaries

## Schema and validation

- Field schema: `terms/SCHEMA.md · tools/SCHEMA.md · models/docs/SCHEMA.md`
- Validator: `terms/scripts/validate.mjs · tools/scripts/validate.mjs · models/scripts/validate.mjs` (non-compliant entries fail the build)
