六个开放权重节点，改写了 2026 的选型表Six open-weight nodes rewrote the 2026 selection table

Each states specs, licence and what it does not claim.

参数、上下文与许可取官方模型卡；能力分数只用第三方公开指数并标注。本站不做模型排名，也不把厂商自评当实测。Parameters, context and licence come from official model cards; capability scores are third-party indices and labelled. This site does not rank models or treat vendor claims as measurement.

为什么值得看Why it matters

它把「1T 级 MoE」与「1M 上下文」放进开放权重，同时保留欧洲许可叙事与多语言覆盖。对需要在本地或私有云部署旗舰模型、又不想完全依赖闭源 API 的团队，这是 2026 年最重要的新增选项之一。It puts a 1T-class MoE and 1M context into open weights while keeping a European licensing story and multilingual coverage. 边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

The release includes an NVFP4 checkpoint and a DSpark draft head, but local hosting still needs multiple GPUs. The licence is modified MIT, not OSI-approved; MaaS use needs a separate grant. AA scores are third-party indices and cannot replace your own task evaluation.

为什么值得看Why it matters

它把「长上下文」的成本从「堆显存」转向「压缓存」，并用 MIT 许可开放权重；对做长文档、代码库与多轮 Agent 的团队，V4.1 Flash 是一个成本敏感型基线。It shifts long-context cost from “more HBM” to “smaller cache” and releases weights under MIT; for long documents, repositories and multi-turn agents, V4.1 Flash is a cost-sensitive baseline.

Native multimodality must be tested per task, not inferred from a spec table. 1017 is a point release; interfaces and behaviour may differ from earlier versions.
