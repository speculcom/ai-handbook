怎么读How to read it

LiveBench 按月刷新题目，用较新的题源降低污染，覆盖数学、代码、推理、数据整理等任务；每个任务单独报准确率。它的重点不是「一次排名」，而是「在新题上是否稳定」。 跨月比较必须先确认版本：题目换了，难度和分布都会变，分数不能直接连成趋势。部分任务用模型评委或规则评分，评委模型换了也会影响结果；报告要写清版本月份、任务子集、评分脚本与评委模型。 它适合做「新鲜度检查」，不适合替代真实业务评测；题量有限，细分任务上的波动可能很大。LiveBench refreshes its items monthly, using newer sources to reduce contamination, and covers maths, code, reasoning, data analysis and other tasks with per-task accuracy. Its point is not one ranking but stability on fresh items. Before comparing across months, confirm the release: when items change, difficulty and distribution change, so scores do not form a trend line. Some tasks use a model judge or rule-based scoring, and swapping the judge changes the result; report the release month, task subset, scoring script and judge model. Use it as a freshness check, not a substitute for your own business evaluation; item counts are limited and sub-task scores can swing.

下一步Next steps

  • 固定题集版本、few-shot 与 CoT 设置，先用公开样例把分数对齐到同一口径。Fix the task-set version, few-shot and CoT settings; align scores on public samples first.
  • 从你自己的知识库抽 50–100 条，补测“知道 / 不知道 / 已过期”三类，而不是只测选择题。Sample 50–100 items from your own corpus and test know / do-not-know / outdated, not only multiple choice.
  • 与另一个知识类基准交叉；单一分数只能说明某类题型的表现，不能代表业务准确率。Cross-check with another knowledge benchmark; one score reflects one task shape, not business accuracy.
