测什么What it measures多轮对话质量Multi-turn dialogue quality
任务与数据Tasks and data80 个多轮问题，覆盖写作、角色扮演、推理等80 multi-turn questions across writing, role-play, reasoning and more
指标Metric模型评委打分（1–10）Model-judge score (1–10)
污染风险Contamination risk题目公开；评委模型可能偏好长回答或特定风格。Medium; prompts are public and judges may favour familiar styles.
复现Reproduction固定评委模型、评分模板与温度；记录单评委还是多评委。Low; mostly judge calls. Fix judge model, prompt and position order.

怎么读How to read it

适合看对话体验，不适合当客观真值；评委偏差要单独报告。Judge scores are not human preferences. Report the judge model and agreement with a small human sample.

下一步Next steps

  • 固定裁判模型、裁判提示与回答位置随机化，先测裁判自身的一致性。Fix the judge model and prompt, randomise answer order, and test the judge’s own consistency first.
  • 双盲人工抽检 100–200 条，把“安全拒答”和“过度拒答”分开统计。Run a blinded human check on 100–200 items, counting safe refusals and over-refusals separately.
  • 报告胜率分布与样本量，不把一个偏好分数当成通用质量分，也不跨榜单直接横比。Report win-rate distribution and sample size; do not treat one preference score as general quality or compare across leaderboards.
