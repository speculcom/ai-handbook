Thinking Machines LabThinking Machines Lab　·　当前代current generation Inkling 2 SmallInkling 2 Small（2026-11）

Thinking Machines · InklingThinking Machines · Inkling模型家族谱系卡model family lineage card

同一代的两条线：Small 是 594B 级旗舰，用来打前沿 Agent / 多模态任务；Mini 是 26B 级本地节点，用循环层换容量。两者都是 Apache-2.0、1M 上下文，但硬件门槛相差一个数量级，不能互相替代。Two lines in the same generation: Small is the 594B-class flagship for frontier agent and multimodal work; Mini is the 26B-class local node that trades repeated layers for capacity. Both are Apache-2.0 with 1M context, but their hardware floors differ by an order of magnitude and they do not replace each other.

这里没有单独的量化系列页：Mini 的 GGUF 档位写进 2026 增补卡，Small 的官方 NVFP4 / MXFP8 权重只作规格记录。No separate quantisation series page: Mini's GGUF tiers are recorded in the 2026 patch card, while Small's official NVFP4 / MXFP8 checkpoints are recorded as specs only.

  • 官方模型卡：MXFP8 checkpoint 需要至少 800GB 聚合显存的 GPU 集群；NVFP4 是另一个更小的 checkpoint。Official model card: the MXFP8 checkpoint requires a GPU cluster with at least 800GB of aggregate VRAM; NVFP4 is a separate, smaller checkpoint.出处 ↗source ↗
  • 官方发布页：Inkling 2 Small 在 AA Intelligence Index 得 31 分，权重于 2026-11-12 在 Hugging Face 以 Apache-2.0 开放。Official release page: Inkling 2 Small scores 31 on the AA Intelligence Index; weights opened on Hugging Face under Apache-2.0 on 2026-11-12.出处 ↗source ↗
  • 官方模型卡：Inkling 2 Mini 为 26.4B 总参 / 3.4B 激活，24 层 decoder-only，6–17 层循环 3 次，1M 上下文与文本 + 图像输入。Official model card: Inkling 2 Mini has 26.4B total / 3.4B active parameters, a 24-layer decoder-only stack with layers 6–17 looped three times, 1M context and text + image input.出处 ↗source ↗
