IBM 的企业向开放权重家族：尺寸克制、许可干净、强调工作流与编码，适合在合规要求高的企业里做私有部署。它不是“跑分最强”路线，优势在许可、可审计与企业集成。IBM’s enterprise-oriented open-weight family: restrained sizes, clean licensing, and an emphasis on workflows and coding, suited to private deployment in compliance-heavy enterprises. It is not a benchmark-maximalist line; its advantage is licensing, auditability and enterprise integration.
