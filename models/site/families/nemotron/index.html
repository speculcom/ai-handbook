NVIDIANVIDIA　·　当前代current generation Nemotron 3.5Nemotron 3.5（2026-11）

NemotronNemotron模型家族谱系卡model family lineage card

NVIDIA 的开放权重 agent 家族：三档 MoE 覆盖单机到集群，主打长上下文、推理与本地 agent。它的价值不在“又一个开源模型”，而在把 NVIDIA 的推理栈、NIM 与硬件适配绑在一起；如果你不用 NVIDIA 硬件，收益会打折。NVIDIA’s open-weight agent family: three MoE tiers from a single machine to a cluster, pitched at long context, reasoning and local agents. Its value is not “another open model” but the coupling with NVIDIA’s inference stack, NIM and hardware; without NVIDIA hardware the benefit is smaller.

本站尚未收录 Nemotron 的量化对比：三档尺寸跨度大，GGUF 生态与下载量还在早期，未达到本站约 30 万下载的门槛。No Nemotron quantisation comparison yet: the three tiers span very different sizes and the GGUF ecosystem is still early, below this site’s ~300k-download threshold.
