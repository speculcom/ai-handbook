这张表只做一件事：把「参数、上下文、许可、能不能在本地跑、大概要多少显存」放在同一屏，省得来回点 20 张家族卡。所有数字来自各家族卡与官方模型卡；显存是按 4-bit 权重的粗算，不含 KV 缓存、视觉编码器与并发。This table does one thing: put parameters, context, licence, local feasibility and approximate VRAM on one screen so you do not have to open 20 family cards. Figures come from the cards and official model cards; VRAM is a rough 4-bit weight estimate and excludes KV cache, vision encoders and concurrency.
