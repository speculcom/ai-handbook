# AI Models (ai-handbook / models)

> For this base model, which quantized build should I download — and why.

Covers **open-weight (distillation included), locally deployable** models with a third-party GGUF quantization ecosystem — whether trained from scratch or distilled. Closed-source / API-only models are out of the quantization scope ("which quantized build" does not apply to them); a separate `data/closed/` set of closed-model lineage cards provides the contrast: official spec snapshots + review cycle, no prices, no rankings.

## Size (computed from data — see the build scripts under `scripts/` and `_audit/gen-repo-docs.mjs`)

11 series / 16 model repos / 129 quant variants / 1270 GGUF files, from 54 quantizers.

## Content map

- `config.js` — Series roster (the selection criteria are in the file-header comments)
- `data/series/*.json` — **Collected output**: series / members / quant repos / variants / sizes — do not edit by hand
- `data/families/*.json` — Family lineage cards (layer-one choice: vendor / generations / size tiers / licences / official claims)
- `data/closed/*.json` — Closed-model lineage cards (the contrast set: official spec snapshots + review cycle; rule D9 records no price figures)
- `guides/*.md` — Model-making guides (methodology articles such as the four training stages; see SCHEMA §model-making guides)
- `data/quantizers/*.md` — Quantizer profiles
- `enums.en.json` — English for the data-layer closed enums
- `summaries.en.json` — Series summaries in English (key = series code)
- `member-notes.en.json` — Member notes in English (key = code|repo name)

## What this repo does not do

- No benchmarks, no quality or speed conclusions
- No downloading or hosting of any model weight files
- No fake rankings (license / size / variant are facts; merits are left to the reader)

## Schema and validation

- Field schema: `docs/SCHEMA.md`
- Validator: `scripts/validate.mjs` (non-compliant entries fail the build)
- A separate legal hard constraint is enforced by `scripts/audit-licenses.mjs` (must pass before the site builds)
