买卡，还是租云？先把三年账算出来Buy a card or rent cloud? Run the three-year numbers

这个计算器不替你选，只把「一次性买卡」和「按小时租」放在同一张账单上。默认值是一张消费级旗舰卡 + 一台主机；换成你手上的报价即可。所有金额为美元，不含税、不含网络与机房。This calculator does not choose for you; it puts an upfront purchase and an hourly rental on the same bill. Defaults are a consumer flagship plus a host; replace them with your quotes. All amounts are USD, excluding tax, network and colocation.

怎么读这几个数How to read the numbers

  • 「买」= 卡价 + 主机 + 电费 + 运维，按年限摊；没有算残值、闲置和折旧税盾。“Buy” = card + host + power + ops over the years; residual value, idle time and tax shields are excluded.
  • 「租」= 云价 × 每天小时 × 365 × 年限；没有算存储、出口流量与长期折扣。“Rent” = cloud rate × hours/day × 365 × years; storage, egress and long-term discounts are excluded.
  • 「平衡点」= 每年要跑多少小时，买才不比租贵。低于它，租更省；高于它，买更省——前提是卡不闲置。“Break-even” = hours per year at which buying costs the same as renting. Below it, rent; above it, buy — provided the card is not idle.
  • 这张账不含「时间」：自己装驱动、修环境、排队和故障排查都要人。把它折算成你的时薪，再加进买卡一侧。This bill excludes time: drivers, environments, queues and troubleshooting cost people. Price your hourly rate and add it to the buy side.
