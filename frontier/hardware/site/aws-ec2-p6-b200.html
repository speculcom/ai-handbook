云算力Cloud compute

AWS EC2 P6-B200AWS EC2 P6-B200

单机 8 卡 Blackwell 的云实例，比 P6e 小一档；1,432 GB 是实例内 8 卡 HBM 合计。3.2 Tb/s 是节点网络上限，跨节点训练仍要看集群网络。页面同时给出 1,440 GB 与表格里的 1,432 GB 两种口径，本站按表格逐实例数字记录并注明差异。An eight-GPU Blackwell cloud instance, one tier below P6e; 1,432 GB is aggregate HBM across the eight GPUs. 3.2 Tb/s is the node network ceiling and cross-node training still depends on cluster fabric. The page gives both 1,440 GB and 1,432 GB in its table; this card records the per-instance table figure and notes the discrepancy.
