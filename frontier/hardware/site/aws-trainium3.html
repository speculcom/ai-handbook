回答「AWS 自研训练芯片最新一代给什么」。Trainium3 是 AWS 首颗 3nm AI 芯片，单颗 2.52 PF FP8、144GB HBM3e、4.9TB/s；Trn3 UltraServer 可扩到 144 颗，合计 362 PF、20.7TB HBM3e、706TB/s，官方称比 Trn2 UltraServer 最多 4.4 倍性能、4 倍每瓦性能。只能通过 EC2 / Neuron SDK 使用，不是通用 GPU；选型时要连实例价格与网络一起算。Answers what AWS’s latest in-house training chip offers. Trainium3 is AWS’s first 3nm AI chip: 2.52 PF at FP8, 144GB of HBM3e and 4.9TB/s per chip; a Trn3 UltraServer scales to 144 chips for 362 PF, 20.7TB of HBM3e and 706TB/s, and AWS claims up to 4.4x the performance and 4x the performance-per-watt of Trn2 UltraServers. It is reachable only through EC2 and the Neuron SDK, not as a general-purpose GPU; compare instance pricing and networking together with the specs.
