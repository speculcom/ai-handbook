NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070

$549$549 · 美国起售价。US starting price.

Blackwell 中端卡：12 GB GDDR7、192-bit、672 GB/s、6,144 CUDA 核心、988 AI TOPS，卡功耗 250 W，建议 650 W 电源，起售价 549 美元。12 GB 能跑 4-bit 8B–14B 级模型，但 30B 4-bit 仍会爆显存；带宽比 5070 Ti 低 25%，token 生成会慢一些。适合 1440p 游戏与入门本地推理，不适合长上下文 30B 或双卡训练。A mid-range Blackwell card: 12 GB GDDR7 on a 192-bit bus, 672 GB/s, 6,144 CUDA cores, 988 AI TOPS, 250 W board power, a 650 W PSU recommendation and a $549 starting price. 12 GB handles 4-bit 8B–14B models, but a 4-bit 30B still runs out of memory; bandwidth is 25% lower than the 5070 Ti, so token generation is slower. Fits 1440p gaming and entry local inference, not long-context 30B or dual-GPU training.
