MemryX MX3+MemryX MX3+

未公布单价No public price未公布单价No public price · 官方未公布单价；MX3+ 尚在 early-access / 工程样片阶段，不拿经销商价冒充官方价。No public unit price; MX3+ is in early-access / engineering-sample stage, so dealer pricing is not presented as official.

回答「MX3+ 比 MX3 大多少、什么时候能用」。官方只给相对倍数：峰值 AI 算力最高 10 倍、片上内存近 10 倍；16 芯片配置合计超过 1 POPS、2.16GB 分布式片上内存。单芯片的绝对 TFLOPS、功耗、内存容量官方尚未公布，所以本卡不拿「10×」乘 MX3 的 6.25 TFLOPS 冒充 MX3+ 规格。工程样片目标 2027 年底，量产目标 2028 年中，现在不能按已上市产品排期。Answers how much larger MX3+ is than MX3 and when it arrives. The vendor gives relative gains only: up to 10x peak AI compute and nearly 10x on-chip memory; a 16-chip configuration is targeted at more than 1 POPS and 2.16 GB of distributed on-chip memory. Per-chip absolute TFLOPS, power and memory capacity are not published, so this card does not multiply MX3's 6.25 TFLOPS by 10 and present that as an MX3+ spec. Engineering samples target late 2027 and volume mid-2028, so it cannot be scheduled as a shipping product today.
