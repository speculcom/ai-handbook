Google TPU 8tx（机密推理）Google TPU 8tx (confidential inference)

未公布单价No public price未公布单价No public price · 官方页未给公开单价；这类产品通常按整机、云实例或询价成交，不拿经销商价冒充官方价。No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

回答「需要在云上处理敏感数据、又不想把数据搬到另一个集群，该看什么加速器」。8tx 复用 8t 硅片，配第五代 AMD EPYC 主机，把机密计算做进加速器硬件层：数据与模型权重在使用中也被隔离保护；同一系统还能做私有数据微调、蒸馏和 RL。
不主张：2× 延迟改善是相对 TPU 8t 的厂商口径，不是独立实测；「near-zero performance penalty」也是 Google 的说法；机密计算不等于合规结论，法律与合同问题仍需单独确认；没有公开价格与第三方吞吐数据。Answers which accelerator to look at when sensitive data must be processed in the cloud without moving it to another cluster. 8tx reuses 8t silicon with 5th-gen AMD EPYC hosts and puts confidential computing into the accelerator hardware layer: data and model weights stay isolated even in use; the same system can also do private-data fine-tuning, distillation and RL.
It does not claim: the 2x latency improvement is a vendor figure versus TPU 8t, not an independent measurement; "near-zero performance penalty" is also Google’s wording; confidential compute is not a compliance conclusion — legal and contractual questions remain separate; no public pricing or third-party throughput data.
