NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72（整机柜）NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 (rack)

未公布单价No public price未公布单价No public price · 官方页未给公开单价；这类产品通常按整机、云实例或询价成交，不拿经销商价冒充官方价。No public unit price on the official page; these usually sell as systems, cloud instances or on request, so dealer quotes are not presented as official.

回答「Rubin 代整机柜到底给多少算力、多少 HBM、什么时候能买到」。NVL72 把 72 颗 Rubin GPU、36 颗 Vera CPU 装进一个 MGX 机柜：NVFP4 推理 3,600 PFLOPS、训练 2,520 PFLOPS，GPU 侧合计 20.7 TB HBM4 / 1,400 TB/s；官方页面标「Available Now」，并称相对 GB200 NVL72 每百万 token 成本降至十分之一、每兆瓦 token 吞吐提升最多 10×。
不主张：算力与成本都是厂商口径，页面自己写「projected performance subject to change」，没有第三方实测；3,600 PF 是 NVFP4 稀疏口径，不能和 FP16 数字直接横比；20.7 TB 是 72 卡合计，不是单卡；「可用」不等于大规模现货。Answers what a Rubin-generation rack delivers in compute, HBM and availability. NVL72 packs 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs into one MGX rack: 3,600 PFLOPS NVFP4 inference, 2,520 PFLOPS training, and 20.7 TB HBM4 / 1,400 TB/s aggregate on the GPU side. The official page marks it "Available Now" and claims one-tenth the cost per million tokens and up to 10x more tokens per megawatt than GB200 NVL72.
It does not claim: compute and cost are vendor figures; the page itself says "projected performance subject to change", with no third-party measurement; 3,600 PF is NVFP4 sparse, not directly comparable with FP16; 20.7 TB is 72-GPU aggregate, not per GPU; "available" does not mean broad volume shipping.
