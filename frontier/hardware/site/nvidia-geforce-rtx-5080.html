NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080

$999$999 · Founders Edition 美国起售价。US MSRP for Founders Edition.

Blackwell 次旗舰：16 GB GDDR7、960 GB/s、10,752 CUDA 核心、1,801 AI TOPS，卡功耗 360 W，建议 850 W 电源。16 GB 跑 4-bit 14B 级模型比较舒服，30B 4-bit 只剩很少上下文；没有 NVLink，多卡只能走 PCIe。适合 4K 游戏加单机中等模型，不适合 30B 以上长上下文或双卡训练。A Blackwell upper-mid card: 16 GB GDDR7, 960 GB/s, 10,752 CUDA cores, 1,801 AI TOPS, 360 W board power and an 850 W PSU recommendation. 16 GB is comfortable for 4-bit 14B-class models; a 4-bit 30B leaves little context. There is no NVLink, so multi-GPU goes over PCIe. Fits 4K gaming plus single-machine mid-size models, not 30B+ long context or dual-GPU training.
