机器人本体Robot bodies

宇树 Go2 四足机器人Unitree Go2 quadruped

回答四足本体的重量、载荷、续航与感知配置，用来判断场地与实验类型。注意三个容易误读的地方：5m/s 是实验室条件；续航 1–4 小时随机型与电池容量变化；工作最大功率约 3000W 属瞬时峰值，不能按此推算平均供电。价格不收录。Answers the weight, payload, endurance and perception setup of a quadruped body, which is what decides the floor space and experiment type it suits. Three figures are easy to misread: 5 m/s is a laboratory figure; endurance ranges from 1 to 4 hours depending on variant and battery; and the ~3000 W working maximum is a transient peak that must not be used to size average power. Price is excluded.
