$3,999$3,999 · 官方开发者套件起售价。Official developer-kit starting price.

回答桌面级统一内存机器的内存、功耗与体积：128GB 统一内存是它和消费级显卡的根本差别，模型不必先切进显存。官方给出的「最多 200B 参数」是推理口径、且为厂商声明，不是实测；多机互联最多 4 台。不回答训练可行性：微调上限官方写作 70B 参数级。Answers the memory, power and footprint of a desktop unified-memory machine: 128 GB of coherent memory is what separates it from consumer graphics cards, since the model need not be staged into dedicated VRAM first. The stated "up to 200B parameters" is an inference figure and a vendor claim rather than a measurement, and at most four units can be linked. It does not answer training feasibility: the vendor puts fine-tuning at up to 70B parameters.
