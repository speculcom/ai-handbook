回答「MI400 家族里给标准 8 卡服务器的是哪一档」。MI440X 是 AMD 的 8 卡服务器 drop-in 型号，配 EPYC Venice；显存与带宽沿用 MI400 系列的 432GB HBM4 / 19.6TB/s，FP4/FP8 分别约 40 / 20 PFLOPS。注意：AMD 尚未单独发布 MI440X 规格页，以上算力与显存数字来自 MI400 系列同代资料与第三方汇总，卡片按「厂商口径」标记，不当作已复核的独立规格。Answers which MI400 family member targets standard 8-GPU servers. MI440X is AMD’s drop-in 8-GPU server part paired with EPYC Venice; it carries the MI400 platform’s 432GB of HBM4 and 19.6TB/s of bandwidth, with roughly 40 / 20 PFLOPS at FP4 / FP8. Caveat: AMD has not published a standalone MI440X spec sheet, so those compute and memory figures come from same-generation MI400 material and third-party summaries; this card marks them as vendor claims, not independently verified specs.
