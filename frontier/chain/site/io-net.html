去中心化推理Decentralised inference

io.net：分布式 GPU 聚合io.net: aggregated distributed GPUs

交集：把独立数据中心、矿业与私有集群的 GPU 聚合起来，按需供给训练/推理；价格与可用性随供给波动。
适用范围：官方称可按需提供 H100/A100 与整簇、分钟级部署、全球分布式网络与可选机密计算；不承诺固定库存或单一区域。
未解决：跨供给方的网络带宽与延迟、数据安全与合规、任务迁移与故障恢复、以及「70% cheaper」这类对比的基准条件需自行核验。Intersection: aggregate GPUs from independent data centers, mining operations and private clusters, then supply them on demand for training/inference; price and availability vary with supply.
Scope: the official site advertises on-demand H100/A100 and full clusters, minute-level deployment, a globally distributed network and optional confidential compute; it does not promise fixed inventory or a single region.
Open: cross-provider bandwidth and latency, data security/compliance, job migration and failure recovery, and the basis of "70% cheaper" comparisons need independent verification.
