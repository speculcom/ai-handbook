核验路径：一条 AI × 链内容该查什么Verification path: what to check on an AI-and-chain claim

顺序按「先分清类型、再回到规范原文、然后看权利与持有、最后看谁能验证」排。每一步只链本站已核过的条目，不给结论也不做推荐；查不到原文就停在那一步，不用二手转述补位。The order is: identify the type first, go back to the specification text, then check rights and custody, and finally who can verify. Each step links only to entries verified here; no conclusions and no recommendations, and if the original text cannot be found the process stops at that step rather than filling in with a second-hand summary.

  1. 1. 先分清这条内容属于哪一类：来源标识、可验证凭证，还是去中心化推理网络——三类查法完全不同。First establish which category the claim belongs to: provenance signalling, verifiable credentials, or a decentralised inference network — the three are checked differently. 条目entry
  2. 2. 如果是内容凭证：查清规范版本与断言类型，并确认 AI 输出用的是哪种来源类型标记。For a content credential: establish the specification version and assertion types, and confirm which source type marks an AI output. 条目entry
  3. 3. 如果声明要离线签名：按类型化结构编码与哈希，不把复杂声明只当成不透明字节串。If a claim needs an offline signature: encode and hash typed structured data instead of treating a complex claim as an opaque byte string. 条目entry
  4. 4. 如果签名来自合约账户：查合约侧签名验证接口，而不是只按个人私钥签名的方式验证。If the signature comes from a contract account: check the contract-side signature validation interface rather than verifying only as an EOA signature. 条目entry
  5. 5. 如果要记录谁按什么 schema 作了证明：查 schema 注册与 attestation 合约，并记住记录不等于内容自动为真。If the claim records who attested what under a schema: check schema registration and attestation contracts, and remember recording does not make the content automatically true. 条目entry
  6. 6. 如果是数据或模型文件：确认它按内容寻址，同一份字节在任何节点得到同一标识。For a dataset or model file: confirm it is content-addressed, so identical bytes yield an identical identifier on any node. 条目entry
  7. 7. 如果出处跨多个文件：查 CID 之上的数据模型与链接方式，不只核单个文件哈希。If provenance spans multiple files: check the data model and linking above CIDs, not only a single file hash. 条目entry
  8. 8. 接着问「现在还有谁持有」：持有证明是周期性的，且不等于随时能快速取回。Then ask who still holds it: the proof of custody is periodic and does not imply fast retrieval. 条目entry
  9. 9. 如果涉及「某机构认证了它」：先分清签发者、持有者、验证者，并记住可验证不等于声明为真。If the claim is that some body attested it: separate issuer, holder and verifier, and remember that verifiable does not mean true. 条目entry
  10. 10. 如果涉及某个标识的控制权：查 DID 文档里的验证方法与撤销方式，撤销规则由各方法自定。If control of an identifier is claimed: check verification methods and revocation in the DID document, noting that revocation rules are per method. 条目entry
  11. 11. 如果要把标识变成可核验材料：执行 DID 解析，取得文档与元数据，再核对目标 method 的实现。To turn an identifier into verifiable material: run DID resolution to obtain the document and metadata, then check the target method implementation. 条目entry
  12. 12. 如果涉及数据能不能用：区分「访问被门控」与「数据合规可用」，后者是法律判断，不是协议能回答的。If data usage is at issue: separate token-gated access from lawful usability — the latter is a legal judgement, not something a protocol answers. 条目entry
  13. 13. 如果私有数据用于训练或评测：区分出售原始数据与授权计算，核对允许算法、计算环境与结果边界。If private data is used for training or evaluation: separate selling raw data from authorised compute, and check allowed algorithms, compute environment and result boundaries. 条目entry
  14. 14. 如果涉及去中心化推理：查清角色分工与打分机制，激励分配由验证者的评分决定，跨子网不可直接比较。For decentralised inference: establish the role split and the scoring mechanism, since incentive distribution follows validator scores and subnets are not directly comparable. 条目entry
