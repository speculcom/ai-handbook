协议档案Protocol records

EIP-4844：Shard Blob TransactionsEIP-4844: Shard Blob Transactions

交集：AI 训练数据、模型卡与内容凭证需要低成本的数据可用性层；blob 把大批数据放到共识层侧车，执行层只读承诺，适合做「可验证但不进 EVM」的存证。
适用范围：官方规范明确这是分片的过渡方案，不是完整分片；blob 数据会被共识节点下载并短期保存，执行层不能直接读取内容；每个区块有目标与上限，费用由独立市场调节。
未解决：长期保存与检索、数据可用性采样、跨链读取证明、以及把 blob 用于训练数据授权时的隐私与许可边界，规范都没有替应用层回答。Intersection: AI training data, model cards and content credentials need a low-cost data-availability layer; blobs put bulk data into consensus-layer sidecars while the execution layer sees only commitments — useful for verifiable records that should not enter the EVM.
Scope: the official spec frames this as a stop-gap before full sharding; blob data is downloaded and briefly retained by consensus nodes, cannot be read directly by EVM execution, and is capped per block with a separate fee market.
Open: long-term retention and retrieval, data-availability sampling, cross-chain proofs, and the privacy/licensing boundary when blobs carry training data are left to the application layer.
