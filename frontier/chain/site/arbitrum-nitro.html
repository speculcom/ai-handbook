Arbitrum Nitro：乐观汇总与交互式欺诈证明Arbitrum Nitro: optimistic rollup with interactive fraud proofs

交集：L2 把执行搬到链下、把数据与结果提交回 L1；Nitro 的四个设计点是「先排序、再确定性执行」「以 Geth 为核心」「执行与证明分离」「乐观汇总 + 交互式欺诈证明」。
适用范围：官方文档说明 Sequencer 负责排序并立即给出结果，batch poster 把压缩批次以 EIP-4844 blob 或 calldata 发回 Ethereum，L1 data fee 补偿这笔成本；AnyTrust 变体用数据可用性委员会降低费用，并保留「委员会失效则回退到 rollup」的路径。
未解决：Sequencer 是中心化排序点，官方明确它拥有「有限的排序权力」；提现挑战期、排序器宕机与 MEV 风险要按具体链的配置读文档，本站不评价任何 L2 的安全等级。The intersection: an L2 moves execution off-chain and posts data and results back to L1; Nitro rests on four ideas — sequencing then deterministic execution, Geth at the core, separating execution from proving, and an optimistic rollup with interactive fraud proofs.
Scope: the docs describe the sequencer ordering transactions and giving immediate results, a batch poster sending compressed batches to Ethereum as EIP-4844 blobs or calldata, and an L1 data fee covering that cost; AnyTrust trades a weaker trust assumption (a data availability committee) for lower fees and falls back to rollup if the committee fails.
Open: the sequencer is a centralised ordering point with, in the docs’ words, limited power over ordering; withdrawal challenge periods, sequencer downtime and MEV depend on each chain’s configuration, and this site does not rank L2 safety.
