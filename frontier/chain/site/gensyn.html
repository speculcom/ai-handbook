去中心化推理Decentralised inference

Gensyn：可审计的去中心化训练Gensyn: auditable decentralised training

交集：把训练过程做成可复现、可审计的执行环境，让外部能验证模型是怎么训出来的，而不是只看最终权重。
适用范围：官方发布 open-1b/REE/open-8b 等可审计训练与可复现执行环境；它是研究/生产平台，不是通用 GPU 租用市场。
未解决：可审计性覆盖到哪一层（数据、代码、随机性、硬件）、大规模任务成本、以及验证者与训练者串谋风险，官方材料未给出完整答案。Intersection: make training reproducible and auditable so outsiders can verify how a model was trained, not just inspect final weights.
Scope: the official site announces open-1b/REE/open-8b and a reproducible execution environment; it is a research/production platform, not a generic GPU rental marketplace.
Open: how deep auditability covers data, code, randomness and hardware; the cost of large runs; and collusion between trainers and verifiers are not fully answered in the public material.
