RAG 与知识库RAG & Knowledge Bases

从文档解析、切分、索引、检索到引用，缺一段都会让答案看起来对、实际不可靠。先定问题，再定语料与评测。Retrieval quality depends on chunking, recall, permissions and evaluation; choose a stack whose sources you can inspect.

20个场景精选selected repos
适合有私有文档、需要引用来源的回答场景；公开知识问答不一定要自建 RAG。For teams grounding answers in private documents and needing citations.适合谁Who it is for

怎么选How to choose

• 想快速把一套知识库跑起来：RAGFlow 或 Dify。Mixed formats and permissions: check parsing, chunking and ACL support first.
• 要自己控检索与生成链路：LlamaIndex / Haystack。Need a ready UI and citations: RAGFlow; code-first pipelines: LlamaIndex.
• 只缺向量检索：Qdrant 生产、Chroma 原型。Measure retrieval separately from generation; keep the source path.
不适合：Not for: RAG 不是把文档丢进模型。文档质量、权限与更新机制比 chunk size 更重要。Do not use RAG to patch missing source data or unclear permissions.
