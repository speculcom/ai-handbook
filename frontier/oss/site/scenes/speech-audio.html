语音与音频Speech & Audio

语音分识别（ASR）与合成（TTS）两条链。先看语言、实时性、说话人分离和授权，再选模型。Transcribe, synthesise and separate speakers: check language coverage, latency, streaming and data handling.

适合会议转写、字幕、配音、语音助手与音频内容生产的人。For meeting notes, voice interfaces and audio archives.适合谁Who it is for

怎么选How to choose

• 多语言转写基线：Whisper；更快更省：faster-whisper。Fast local transcription: Whisper variants; streaming: check chunking and VAD.
• 中文会议与标点：FunASR；词级时间戳：WhisperX。Speaker labels and timestamps: test on your own recordings.
• 中文 TTS：CosyVoice；少样本克隆：GPT-SoVITS（必须授权）。Voice cloning needs explicit consent and an abuse-review path.
不适合：Not for: 声音克隆不是玩具。没有本人授权，不要克隆、不要冒充、不要商用。Do not upload confidential audio to a service you have not reviewed.
