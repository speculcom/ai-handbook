评测与可观测Evaluation & Observability

上线前要能测，上线后要能看。评测管“答得对不对”，可观测管“为什么慢、为什么贵、哪一步失败”。Measure quality and trace failures: choose tools that record prompts, retrieval, tool calls, latency and cost.

适合要把 LLM 应用从 demo 推到生产的人；一次性脚本不需要整套平台。For teams shipping agents or RAG and needing regression tests.适合谁Who it is for

怎么选How to choose

• RAG 回归：Ragas；像单测一样测：DeepEval。Start with a small labelled set and a failure taxonomy.
• 多模型 / 多 prompt 对比：promptfoo。Trace every step: Langfuse and similar tools; offline scoring: promptfoo.
• 追踪与成本：Langfuse / Phoenix；上线前红队：Giskard。Track cost and latency beside quality; a score alone is not a release gate.
不适合：Not for: 榜单一分不代表你的场景可用。先建自己的 50–100 条评测集，再谈平台。Do not optimise a dashboard before defining what a failure looks like.
