图像与视频生成的工具分两层：工作流界面（ComfyUI / WebUI）和代码库（Diffusers）。先看你要创作还是集成。Generate and edit images or video: check model licence, VRAM, input limits and commercial-use terms.

适合做视觉内容、产品图、视频素材与多模态应用的人；只要文字生成的不需要。For visual content teams that need repeatable pipelines, not one-off prompts.适合谁Who it is for

怎么选How to choose

• 复杂工作流、可复用节点：ComfyUI。Image editing with masks: ComfyUI; video generation: check duration and resolution limits.
• 新手少调参：Fooocus；经典界面：SD WebUI。Review output rights and training-data claims before shipping.
• 要把生成嵌进应用：Diffusers；视频生成：Wan2.1。Keep a human review step for faces, text and brand assets.
不适合：Not for: 模型与工作流都有版权和肖像权问题；商用前逐项核对，不能默认可商用。Do not treat a demo output as a production licence.
