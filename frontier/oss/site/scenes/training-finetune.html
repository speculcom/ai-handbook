训练与微调Training & Fine-tuning

把现成模型改成你自己的：从单卡 QLoRA 到多机 RLHF，先看显存、并行方式与可复现性。Turn a base model into your own: from single-GPU QLoRA to multi-node RLHF, start with VRAM, parallelism and reproducibility.

适合要改模型行为、做领域适配或对齐训练的人；只做推理不用进这个场景。For people who need to change model behavior, adapt to a domain or run alignment training; inference-only work does not need this scene.适合谁Who it is for

怎么选How to choose

• 单卡 24GB 想微调 7B：先看 Unsloth。24 GB single GPU, 7B model: start with Unsloth.
• 想把微调流程固化成配置：Axolotl。Turn fine-tuning into a reproducible config: Axolotl.
• 做 DPO/PPO/GRPO 对齐：TRL 起步，规模化再看 verl / OpenRLHF。DPO/PPO/GRPO alignment: start with TRL, scale with verl / OpenRLHF.
• 要训大模型或多机：DeepSpeed、TorchTitan。Large-model or multi-node training: DeepSpeed, TorchTitan.
• MoE 本地跑不动：KTransformers 的专家卸载。MoE does not fit locally: KTransformers expert offload.
不适合：Not for: 不要为了“全参训练”而全参；多数领域适配 LoRA/QLoRA 就够，先算数据量与评测收益。Do not choose full-parameter training by default; most domain adaptation is served by LoRA/QLoRA. Estimate data volume and eval gain first.
