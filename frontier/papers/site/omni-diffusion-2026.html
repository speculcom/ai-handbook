多模态Multimodal

Omni-Diffusion: Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation with Masked Discrete DiffusionOmni-Diffusion: Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation with Masked Discrete Diffusion

问题：多模态大模型几乎都押在自回归骨架上，离散扩散作为统一理解与生成的替代路线仍缺一个完整的任意模态系统。
证据位置：摘要称 Omni-Diffusion 是首个完全基于掩码离散扩散的任意到任意多模态语言模型，统一文本、语音、图像的输入与输出；在多项基准上优于或持平已有多模态系统（含处理两种以上模态的系统）；论文被 ICML 2026 接收。
作者自陈局限：覆盖模态只有文本、语音、图像，没有视频、3D、动作；「首个」是作者主张，未见独立复核；基准持平不等于全场景更强；未报告生产部署与推理成本。
它没主张的：不是自回归的全面替代；不是视频/3D 模型；不是已证明更省算力；不是所有任务都更好。Problem: multimodal LLMs overwhelmingly rely on an autoregressive backbone; discrete diffusion remains an alternative route for unified understanding and generation but lacks a complete any-to-any system.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract describes Omni-Diffusion as the first any-to-any multimodal language model built entirely on mask-based discrete diffusion, unifying text, speech and image inputs and outputs; it outperforms or matches existing multimodal systems, including systems that handle two or more modalities, on a set of benchmarks; the paper is accepted to ICML 2026.
What the authors say about limits: coverage is text, speech and images only, with no video, 3D or action; “first” is the authors’ claim and has not been independently checked; benchmark parity is not across-the-board superiority; production deployment and inference cost are not reported.
What it does not claim: a wholesale replacement for autoregression; a video or 3D model; proof of lower compute; better results on every task.
