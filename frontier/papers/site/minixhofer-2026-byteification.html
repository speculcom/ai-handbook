Retrofitting language models to operate over bytes（Byteification）Retrofitting language models to operate over bytes (Byteification)

问题：主流 LLM 先把文本切成子词，字符级信息被遮住；代码、生物序列这类「单个字符/字节有意义」的数据尤其吃亏。字节级模型能避开分词，但此前性能一直落后。
证据位置：摘要给出方法与 49.1B token（不到典型预训练预算 1%）的转换成本；正文「Tokenizer transfer and retrofitting」说明两阶段流程；结果段报告 Bolmo 7B/1B、Bwen 8B、Blama 8B 等转换模型在字符级推理上超过早期字节级方法，并给出实际推理速度。
作者自陈局限：49.1B token 虽不到 1%，绝对量仍大；方法依赖一个已有子词模型作为教师，换源模型要重做；字节级推理需要专门内核，通用框架的支持并不自动成立；论文没有覆盖所有任务类型。
它没主张的：不是从零训练字节级模型；不是说字节级在所有任务上超过子词模型；不是宣布 tokenizer 无用；不是给出任意模型都能低成本转换的保证。Problem: mainstream LLMs split text into subwords first, hiding character-level information; code and biological sequences, where individual characters or bytes matter, suffer most. Byte-level models avoid tokenisation but had lagged in performance.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract gives the method and the 49.1B-token conversion cost (less than 1% of a typical pretraining budget); the “Tokenizer transfer and retrofitting” section describes the two-stage procedure; the results report Bolmo 7B/1B, Bwen 8B and Blama 8B and character-level reasoning gains over earlier byte-level approaches, plus practical inference speed.
What the authors say about limits: 49.1B tokens is under 1% but still large in absolute terms; the method needs an existing subword model as teacher, so changing the source means redoing it; byte-level inference needs dedicated kernels, and generic framework support is not automatic; the paper does not cover every task type.
What it does not claim: training a byte-level model from scratch; that byte-level models beat subword models on every task; that tokenisers are obsolete; or that any model can be converted cheaply.
