六篇论文，六句「它没主张什么」Six papers, six “what it does not claim” notes

Edit-based training 只监督首个失败点；高风险目标建模检查决策依赖；Byteification 用 49.1B token 把子词模型改字节级；JEPA-PRH 用 109M 模型检验跨模态对齐；识别—拒答错位把「知道不可答却答了」解释为路由失败；Abstain-R1 用 3B 模型做可校准弃答。Edit-based training supervises only the first failure; high-stakes target formulation checks decision dependency; Byteification converts subword models to bytes with 49.1B tokens; JEPA-PRH tests cross-modal alignment at 109M; recognition–refusal explains “knowing it is unanswerable yet answering” as routing failure; Abstain-R1 does calibrated abstention at 3B.

论文站只收能落到原文的条目；每篇都写问题、证据位置、作者自陈局限、它没主张的。预印本与同行评审分开标。Only entries with text-level evidence; each states the problem, evidence location, author-stated limits and what it does not claim. Preprints and peer review are labelled separately.

2026-11-19arXiv / 训练arXiv / training

Edit-based training：只教第一个失败点Edit-based training: supervise only the first failure

不再让模型模仿整条推理轨迹，也不只给整条轨迹一个奖励：编辑器先找出「第一个不符合要求的点」，只对最小替换片段做监督。方法可离线也可在线，在线时监督会跟着模型当前失败模式移动。作者在 Qwen3-32B 上用 ReasonIF 验证。Instead of imitating a full reasoning trajectory or rewarding the whole completion, an editor locates the first point of failure and supervises only a minimal replacement span. The method works offline or online, with supervision moving to the model’s current failure modes. The authors test it on Qwen3-32B with ReasonIF.

摘要报告：相对全轨迹模仿与轨迹级 RL，edit-based training 明显提升对推理指令的遵循，同时大体保留未目标任务的能力；训练信号集中在失败点后的最小替换片段。The abstract reports that, versus full-trajectory imitation and trajectory-level RL, edit-based training substantially improves compliance with reasoning instructions while largely preserving untargeted task skills; supervision concentrates on the minimal replacement span after the failure point.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

只验证 Qwen3-32B 与 ReasonIF 一个基准，不能外推到所有模型与任务；「第一个失败点」依赖编辑器与判定标准，编辑质量决定上限；论文没有给生产规模或跨语言结果。它没主张「替代 RLHF」或「所有推理任务都适用」。Only Qwen3-32B and one benchmark, ReasonIF, are tested; this does not generalise to all models or tasks. The “first failure” depends on the editor and criterion, and edit quality caps the gain; the paper gives no production-scale or cross-lingual results. It does not claim to replace RLHF or to work for every reasoning task.

2026-11-18arXiv / 高风险决策arXiv / high-stakes decisions

LLM 会把预测目标建对吗？Do LLMs formulate the right prediction target?

论文考察 LLM 在儿童福利、医疗、就业等高风险评估里能否正确构造预测问题，重点是「决策依赖」：历史决策会影响被观察到的结果。作者测试 11 个 OpenAI / Anthropic 模型、7 种提示条件，检查生成代码与解释是否处理这一偏差。The paper asks whether LLMs correctly formulate prediction problems in child welfare, healthcare and employment, focusing on decision dependency: past decisions shape observed outcomes. It tests 11 OpenAI / Anthropic models under seven prompt conditions, checking whether generated code and explanations handle that bias.

摘要：没有提示引导时，LLM 不能可靠处理决策依赖；当提示明确写出目标、所需假设与估计步骤时，目标对齐实现达到 99–100%。前沿模型下，简单描述「决策如何影响结果」似乎足以触发正确实现。Abstract: without guidance, LLMs do not reliably address decision dependency; when the prompt specifies the target, the required assumption and the estimation procedure, target-aligned implementation reaches 99–100%. Among frontier models, a simple description of how decisions affect outcomes appears sufficient.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

测试集中在三个高风险领域与特定提示，不等于所有数据分析；99–100% 是「目标对齐实现」的比例，不是预测准确性或部署效果；部分回答口头承认决策依赖、代码却没处理。它没主张「LLM 可自动做高风险建模」或「提示工程可替代方法学审查」。The tests cover three high-stakes domains and specific prompts, not all data analysis; 99–100% is the share of target-aligned implementations, not predictive accuracy or deployment outcomes; some answers acknowledge decision dependency in prose while failing to handle it in code. It does not claim LLMs can automate high-stakes modelling, or that prompting replaces methodological review.

2026-10-29arXiv / 表征arXiv / representation

JEPA-PRH：109M 文本 JEPA 的跨模态对齐JEPA-PRH: cross-modal alignment from a 109M text JEPA

一个 109M 参数的文本 JEPA，在跨模态对齐上优于作者评测过的所有语言模型（最大到 72B）；「好视角」比 next-token 少 42 倍训练样本。A 109M-parameter text JEPA aligns with image encoders better than every language model the authors evaluated, up to 72B; “good views” need 42 times fewer training samples than next-token prediction.

摘要报告 109M JEPA 与 DINOv2 / CLIP 的对齐结果，以及 42× 样本效率；正文用 JEPA 作为可证明/可改进的实例。The abstract reports alignment with DINOv2 / CLIP and the 42× sample efficiency; the body uses JEPA as a provable/improveable instance.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

预印本；109M 远小于被比较的 LLM；对齐指标不是生成质量；42× 是特定视角与数据条件，不是通用常数。它没主张「小模型全面超过大模型」或「PRH 已被最终证明」。A preprint; 109M is far smaller than the compared LLMs; alignment is not generation quality; 42× is specific to views and data, not a universal constant. It does not claim small models beat large models across the board, or that PRH is finally proved.

2026-10-07Nature / 架构Nature / architecture

Byteification：把子词模型改成字节级Byteification: retrofitting subword models to bytes

用不到典型预训练预算 1% 的 49.1B token，把一个已有子词 LLM 转成字节级；报告 Bolmo 7B/1B、Bwen 8B、Blama 8B 在字符级推理上超过早期字节级方法。Converts an existing subword LLM to byte level with 49.1B tokens, less than 1% of a typical pretraining budget; reports Bolmo 7B/1B, Bwen 8B and Blama 8B beating earlier byte-level approaches on character-level reasoning.

摘要给出方法与 49.1B token 的转换成本；正文「Tokenizer transfer and retrofitting」说明两阶段流程；结果段报告字符级任务与实际推理速度。The abstract gives the method and the 49.1B-token conversion cost; the “Tokenizer transfer and retrofitting” section describes the two-stage procedure; the results report character-level tasks and practical inference speed.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

49.1B 虽不到 1%，绝对量仍大；方法依赖已有子词模型当教师；字节级推理需要专门内核；论文不覆盖所有任务。它没主张「字节级全面超过子词」或「tokenizer 无用」。49.1B is under 1% but still large in absolute terms; the method needs an existing subword teacher; byte-level inference needs dedicated kernels; the paper does not cover every task. It does not claim byte-level beats subword everywhere, or that tokenisers are obsolete.

2026EMNLP / 推理EMNLP / reasoning

识别—拒答错位：知道不可答，却还是答了Recognition–refusal misalignment: knowing it is unanswerable, yet answering

论文在隐藏状态里找到一条线性方向，能把可答与「结构上不可能」的数学/代码题分开；识别方向与安全拒答方向接近正交，因此把失败解释为路由问题，而不是编码问题。The paper finds a linear direction in hidden states separating answerable from structurally impossible math/code prompts; recognition is nearly orthogonal to safety refusal, so the failure is a routing problem, not an encoding problem.

线性探针能分开两类题；识别方向与拒答方向近正交。A linear probe separates the two classes; the recognition and refusal directions are nearly orthogonal.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

只覆盖数学/代码的结构性不可答，不是所有「不知道」；线性可分不等于生成时一定使用该方向；未给通用修复方案。它没主张「所有幻觉都是路由问题」或「加一个拒答头就能解决」。It covers structurally unanswerable math/code, not every “don’t know”; linear separability does not prove the model uses that direction when generating; no general fix is offered. It does not claim every hallucination is routing, or that a refusal head solves it.

Abstain-R1：3B 模型的可校准弃答Abstain-R1: calibrated abstention at 3B

用 clarification-aware RLVR 奖励训练 3B 模型，在不可答查询上同时改善弃答与澄清，并保持可答查询上的表现。Trains a 3B model with a clarification-aware RLVR reward, improving abstention and clarification on unanswerable queries while preserving answerable-query performance.

边界 / 它没主张什么Boundary / what it does not claim

3B 规模，不能外推到 70B+；任务以不可答查询为主，开放域多轮澄清覆盖有限；奖励依赖可验证标签，标签有噪声；无生产验证。它没主张「弃答越多越好」或「解决所有幻觉」。3B scale, not extrapolable to 70B+; tasks centre on unanswerable queries, with limited open-domain multi-turn coverage; rewards depend on verifiable labels that carry noise; no production validation. It does not claim more abstention is always better, or that all hallucinations are solved.
