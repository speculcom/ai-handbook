MobileWan: Closing the Quality Gap for Mobile Video DiffusionMobileWan: Closing the Quality Gap for Mobile Video Diffusion

问题：视频扩散靠把 Transformer 扩到数十亿参数提升画质，但移动端模型仍困在 0.4–1.8B 参数，质量上不去。
证据位置：摘要反过来主张“高质量移动生成不必小模型”：从 Wan2.2-5B 出发，用递归蒸馏把生成改成 chunk 级自回归、常数显存注意力；结合因果线性注意力，推理时像 RNN 且保持跨 chunk 时序；再用二值 per-head gate 的可学习注意力头剪枝（噪声偏置稀疏目标 + 蒸馏微调）、采样步蒸馏与显存优化 VAE 解码；首款 5B 级商用手机可部署，5 秒 480×832、16 FPS、端到端 20 秒，VBench 83.79。
作者自陈局限：依赖 Qualcomm 硬件与特定推理栈；5 秒/480×832 是较小规格；20 秒端到端不等于交互式；VBench 83.79 是自动分；训练蒸馏管线复杂；真实手机型号/内存门槛未在摘要给全。
它没主张的：不是通用移动视频模型；不是 5B 一定优于小模型；不是所有手机都能跑。Problem: video diffusion has improved by scaling transformer architectures to billions of parameters, while mobile models remain limited to 0.4–1.8B and lag in quality.
Where the evidence sits: starting from Wan2.2-5B, recurrence distillation turns generation into a chunk-wise autoregressive process with constant-memory attention; causal linear attention makes inference RNN-like while preserving cross-chunk coherence; learnable per-head binary gates prune attention heads with a noise-biased sparsity objective and distillation finetuning; with sampling-step distillation and memory-optimised VAE decoding, it is the first 5B-scale video diffusion model deployable on a commercial phone, generating 5-second 480×832 at 16 FPS in 20 seconds end-to-end with VBench 83.79.
What the authors say about limits: depends on Qualcomm hardware and a specific inference stack; 5 seconds at 480×832 is a modest spec; 20 seconds end-to-end is not interactive; VBench 83.79 is automatic; the distillation pipeline is complex; the exact phone models and memory threshold are not fully given in the abstract.
What it does not claim: a general mobile video model; that 5B always beats smaller models; that every phone can run it.
