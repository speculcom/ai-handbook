From Inference Engine to Inference Control Plane: Connecting vLLM, llm-d, and the Evolution of Efficient Distributed LLM ServingFrom Inference Engine to Inference Control Plane: Connecting vLLM, llm-d, and the Evolution of Efficient Distributed LLM Serving

问题：LLM 推理正从“引擎内优化”变成“跨集群的分布式控制”：可复用状态、阶段放置、异构加速器、网络、自动扩缩、可靠性和 SLO 都要一起管。
证据位置：摘要明确这是综述/研究议程，不是新基准；把 vLLM（PagedAttention、连续批处理、kernel、量化、并行）与 llm-d（控制面）视为互补层，提出 Inference Execution Planner 选择聚合/分离拓扑、KV 来源与传输、硬件变体、路由/准入和扩缩策略；所有性能数字均归原出处。
作者自陈局限：没有新实验测量；结论依赖被引系统的公开数据与生产报告；控制面抽象仍缺统一接口与故障模型；SLO-goodput 评估框架尚未在真实集群验证。
它没主张的：不是 vLLM/llm-d 的性能对比；不是一套可直接部署的调度器；不是“控制面一定优于引擎优化”。Problem: LLM inference is shifting from engine-local optimisation to distributed control over reusable state, phase placement, heterogeneous accelerators, networking, autoscaling, reliability and SLOs.
Where the evidence sits: explicitly a synthesis and research agenda, not a new benchmark; vLLM (PagedAttention, continuous batching, kernels, quantisation, parallelism) and llm-d (control plane) are treated as complementary layers; an Inference Execution Planner selects aggregated vs disaggregated topology, KV source and transfer, hardware variant, routing/admission and scaling policy; every performance number stays attributed to its original source.
What the authors say about limits: no new experimental measurements; conclusions rest on cited public data and production reports; the control-plane abstraction still lacks a unified interface and failure model; the SLO-goodput evaluation framework is not validated on a real cluster.
What it does not claim: a vLLM vs llm-d performance comparison; a deployable scheduler; that a control plane always beats engine-level optimisation.
