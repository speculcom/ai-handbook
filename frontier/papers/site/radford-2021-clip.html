CLIP：用自然语言监督学习可迁移的视觉模型Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision

问题：视觉模型通常预测固定类别，换一个概念就要重新标数据；能不能直接用互联网图文对做对比学习，让自然语言成为分类器？
证据位置：方法在 §2（对比学习、zero-shot 分类器构造）；分析在 §3（细粒度、计数、OCR、分布偏移等）；局限集中在 §6，作者把散落在各节的问题汇总成一份清单。
作者自陈局限：§6 写得非常直白——zero-shot CLIP 平均只是接近「ResNet-50 特征 + 线性分类器」这一简单基线，要达到整体 SOTA 估计还需要约 1000 倍算力，当前硬件不可行；在细粒度分类（车型、花种、飞机型号）、计数、以及训练分布外的新任务上很弱，MNIST 手写数字只有 88%，不如原始像素上的逻辑回归；只能从给定分类器里的概念中选，不能像 caption 模型那样生成新描述；没有解决数据效率问题；开发过程中反复在完整验证集上调参，不符合真正 zero-shot 的设定；评测集是「有点随意拼出来的 27 个数据集」，与 CLIP 能力共同演化；训练数据未筛选，会学到社会偏见。
它没主张的：没有说 zero-shot 已经全面超过监督模型——原文说「often competitive」；没有说 CLIP 能处理所有视觉概念——§6 明确说很多任务接近随机；也没有说 4 亿图文对是数据效率的解法——作者反而说它只是用规模换监督。Problem: vision models usually predict a fixed label set and need new labels for every new concept; can internet image-text pairs be used for contrastive pretraining so natural language itself becomes the classifier?
Where the evidence sits: approach in Section 2 (contrastive pretraining and zero-shot classifier construction); analysis in Section 3 (fine-grained, counting, OCR, distribution shift); limitations are collected in Section 6.
Stated limits: Section 6 is unusually direct — zero-shot CLIP is on average only competitive with a linear classifier on ResNet-50 features; reaching overall state of the art is estimated to need about 1000x more compute, infeasible on current hardware; it is weak on fine-grained classification (car models, flower species, aircraft variants), counting, and novel out-of-distribution tasks, scoring only 88% on MNIST handwritten digits, below logistic regression on raw pixels; it can only choose among concepts in a given classifier, unlike a caption model that can generate novel descriptions; it does not solve data efficiency; development repeatedly queried full validation sets, which is unrealistic for true zero-shot; the 27-dataset evaluation collection was somewhat haphazardly assembled and co-adapted with CLIP; and unfiltered training pairs teach social biases.
What it does not claim: it does not claim zero-shot beats supervised models across the board — the paper says “often competitive”; it does not claim CLIP handles all visual concepts — Section 6 says many tasks are near chance; and it does not claim 400M pairs solve data efficiency, arguing instead that CLIP trades scale for supervision.
