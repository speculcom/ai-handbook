对齐Alignment

Abstain-R1: Calibrated Abstention and Post-Refusal Clarification via Verifiable RLAbstain-R1: Calibrated Abstention and Post-Refusal Clarification via Verifiable RL

问题：模型要么硬答、要么一句「无法回答」结束；缺的是「该弃答时弃答，弃答后还能问清缺什么」的可校准行为。
证据位置：摘要报告一个 3B 模型，用 clarification-aware RLVR 奖励，在不可答查询上同时改善弃答与澄清，并保持可答查询上的表现；方法属于可验证奖励的强化学习，不是提示词模板。
作者自陈局限：3B 规模，不能直接外推到 70B+；训练/评测任务以不可答查询为主，开放域闲聊与多轮澄清覆盖有限；奖励设计依赖可验证的答案/澄清标签，标签本身有噪声；没有生产部署验证。
它没主张的：不是通用助手模型；不是解决所有幻觉；不是「弃答越多越好」；不是不需要人工评估的自动对齐方案。Problem: models either answer anyway or stop with “cannot answer”; what is missing is calibratable behaviour that abstains when appropriate and then asks for the missing information.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a 3B model trained with a clarification-aware RLVR reward that improves abstention and clarification on unanswerable queries while preserving strong performance on answerable ones; it is verifiable-reward RL, not a prompt template.
What the authors say about limits: 3B scale, not directly extrapolable to 70B+; training and evaluation centre on unanswerable queries, with limited coverage of open-domain chat and multi-turn clarification; the reward depends on verifiable answer/clarification labels, which carry noise; no production deployment is reported.
What it does not claim: a general assistant model; a solution to all hallucinations; that more abstention is always better; an auto-alignment method that needs no human evaluation.
