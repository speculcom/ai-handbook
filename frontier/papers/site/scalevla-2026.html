机器人Robotics

ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion ParametersScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters

问题：前沿 VLM 已显示机器人控制潜力，但多数 VLA 策略仍用很小的 VLM 骨干，没吃到规模红利。
证据位置：摘要把 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 骨干接到可训练 action DiT，在统一训练设置下比较 2B–552B；样本效率上 10 条示范超过 2B 用 50 条（87.8% vs 85.6%）；LIBERO-PRO 成功率 45.1%，小骨干最多 31.3%；真实充电器插入从 4/10 到 9/10；每 action chunk 中位延迟 216 ms。
作者自陈局限：552B 训练/推理成本极高，不是普通实验室可复现；只测三类能力与有限任务；216 ms 中位延迟是否满足高频控制仍未展开；依赖 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 权重与许可；真实世界任务数量少。
它没主张的：不是通用机器人基础模型；不是“参数越大越好”；不是实时闭环控制已达标。Problem: frontier VLMs show robot-control potential, but most VLA policies still use much smaller VLM backbones and leave that scale untapped.
Where the evidence sits: a 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash backbone is connected to a trainable action DiT and compared with 2B–552B under one training setup; 10 demonstrations beat a 2B backbone trained on 50 (87.8% vs 85.6%); LIBERO-PRO reaches 45.1% versus at most 31.3% for smaller backbones; real charger insertion rises from 4/10 to 9/10; median latency is 216 ms per action chunk.
What the authors say about limits: 552B training and inference are prohibitively expensive and not reproducible in an ordinary lab; only three capability families and a limited task set are tested; whether 216 ms meets high-frequency control is not analysed; depends on DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash weights and licence; few real-world tasks.
What it does not claim: a general robot foundation model; that bigger is always better; that real-time closed-loop control is solved.
