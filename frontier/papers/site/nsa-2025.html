Native Sparse Attention: Hardware-Aligned and Natively Trainable Sparse AttentionNative Sparse Attention: Hardware-Aligned and Natively Trainable Sparse Attention

问题：长上下文的标准注意力成本随长度平方增长；稀疏注意力往往只在推理阶段可用，或速度提升被内存访问拖掉，能不能做成可端到端预训练的硬件友好稀疏注意力。
证据位置：摘要提出 NSA，用粗粒度 token 压缩加细粒度 token 选择的分层稀疏策略；算法与硬件对齐，支持端到端训练；在 64k 长度上，解码、前向、反向都相对 Full Attention 有明显加速；预训练模型在通用、长上下文与指令推理基准上保持或超过 Full Attention。
作者自陈局限：主要证据在 64k 长度；加速依赖具体硬件与 kernel 实现；评测以预训练模型为主，未覆盖所有下游与部署场景；没有独立复现。
它没主张的：不是无限长度都线性；不是所有 GPU 上都能拿到同样加速；不是稀疏化一定无损。Problem: standard attention scales quadratically with context; sparse attention is often inference-only or loses its speedup to memory access, so can it be made hardware-aligned and natively trainable end to end.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract introduces NSA with a dynamic hierarchical sparse strategy combining coarse token compression and fine token selection; it is hardware-aligned and end-to-end trainable; at 64k length it substantially speeds up decoding, forward and backward passes versus full attention; pretrained models maintain or exceed full attention on general, long-context and instruction-reasoning benchmarks.
What the authors say about limits: main evidence is at 64k; speedups depend on specific hardware and kernel implementations; evaluation centres on pretrained models, not every downstream or deployment case; there is no independent reproduction.
What it does not claim: not linear for unlimited length; not the same speedup on every GPU; not that sparsification is always lossless.
