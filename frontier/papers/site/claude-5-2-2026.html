Introducing Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2Introducing Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2

问题：Fable 5.2 与 Mythos 5.2 同底座，为什么分成两档，安全边界怎么划。
证据位置：官方发布页写明 Fable 带网络安全与生物安全分类器，会拦下多数高风险网络任务；Mythos 仅向经过验证的机构开放，主打网络安全工程；两者共用同一底座，系统卡另附。
作者自陈局限：安全结论来自 Anthropic 自己的预发布测试与内部评测；没有独立第三方复现；部分能力（如 AI 研发加速）在训练时被刻意限制，相关任务只到 Opus 5.5 水平。
它没主张的：不是「无护栏的更强模型」；不是所有机构都能用 Mythos；不是对全部网络攻防任务都开放。Problem: Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 share one underlying model — why split them, and where is the safety boundary?
Where the evidence sits: the official announcement says Fable carries cyber and biology classifiers that block most high-risk cyber tasks; Mythos is limited to vetted organizations and focuses on cybersecurity engineering; both share one underlying model, with a separate system card.
What the authors say about limits: safety conclusions come from Anthropic’s own prerelease testing and internal evaluations, with no independent third-party reproduction; some capabilities, such as accelerating AI research, were deliberately limited during training and perform only at Opus 5.5 level.
What it does not claim: an unguarded “stronger model”; that every organization can use Mythos; that it is open for all cyber offence and defence tasks.
