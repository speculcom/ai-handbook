规模律Scaling laws

Llama 2：开放基础模型与对话微调模型Llama 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models

问题：开放权重模型能不能在有用性与安全性上接近闭源模型，同时把训练、微调与安全对齐的做法公开到可复用的程度？
证据位置：预训练在 §2（数据、40% 增量、双倍上下文、GQA）；微调在 §3（SFT、拒绝采样、PPO）；安全在 §4；观察与洞察在 §5，其中 §5.2 是 Limitations and Ethical Considerations；结论在 §7。
作者自陈局限：§5.2 列得很全——知识在预训练后停止更新；可能生成不实内容，例如不合格建议；有幻觉倾向；训练以英文为主，其他语言能力脆弱；可能生成有害、冒犯或偏见内容；安全微调有时过度保守，会出现过多拒答或堆砌安全说明；安全基准本身有局限，未必覆盖对抗输入、毒性内容与所有人群。摘要还说明 34B 模型因「没有足够时间做红队测试」而暂不发布。
它没主张的：没有说 Llama 2-Chat 在所有基准上超过闭源模型——原文是「may be a suitable substitute」，且基于自家人类评估；没有说安全对齐已经解决——§5.2 明说部分问题仍可能存在；也没有说多语言可用——原文说英文以外「fragile and should be used with caution」。Problem: can an open-weight model approach closed models on helpfulness and safety while publishing pretraining, fine-tuning and safety alignment in a reusable way?
Where the evidence sits: pretraining in Section 2 (data, 40% more tokens, doubled context, GQA); fine-tuning in Section 3 (SFT, rejection sampling, PPO); safety in Section 4; observations in Section 5, with 5.2 covering limitations and ethical considerations; conclusion in Section 7.
Stated limits: 5.2 is thorough — knowledge stops updating after pretraining; the model can generate non-factual content such as unqualified advice; it is prone to hallucination; training is predominantly English and other languages are fragile; it may produce harmful, offensive or biased content; safety tuning sometimes over-corrects into excessive refusal or too much safety boilerplate; and safety benchmarks themselves may miss adversarial inputs, toxic content and some demographic groups. The abstract also says the 34B model was withheld for lack of time to red-team it sufficiently.
What it does not claim: it does not claim Llama 2-Chat beats closed models on all benchmarks — the paper says it “may be a suitable substitute” based on its own human evaluations; it does not claim safety alignment is solved — 5.2 says some issues may remain; and it does not claim multilingual readiness, calling non-English performance fragile and to be used with caution.
