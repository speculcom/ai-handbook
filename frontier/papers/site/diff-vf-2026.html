生成媒体Generative media

Diff-VF: Training-free High-quality Long Video Generation via Diffusion ModelDiff-VF: Training-free High-quality Long Video Generation via Diffusion Model

问题：多数视频扩散模型只用短视频训练，外推到长视频时退化：长时间一致性保不住，动作多样性也丢。
证据位置：摘要提出 Diff-VF，免训练、即插即用、模型无关，把现成短视频骨干变长视频生成器，不改/不微调基座；三个策略：Hybrid Noise Initialization 约束全局语义、Weighted Window Sampling 消除窗口接缝、Temporal Extended Sampling 用随 timestep 变化的融合建立长程依赖；再用 Skip Residual Guidance 做长视频增强；VBench-Long 上在时序一致性与动作多样性之间比 FreeNoise/FreeLong/RIFLEx 更均衡，并在两个基座验证。
作者自陈局限：仍是推理期技巧，不增加模型知识；窗口/融合超参依赖基座；VBench-Long 是自动指标，缺人类偏好；长视频长度上限未给；没有实时性/显存报告。
它没主张的：不是新的长视频训练方法；不是所有基座都同等受益；不是超越专门训练的长视频模型。Problem: most video diffusion models are trained on short clips and degrade when extrapolated to long video, losing long-range temporal coherence or motion diversity.
Where the evidence sits: Diff-VF is training-free, plug-and-play and model-agnostic, turning existing short-video backbones into long-video generators without modifying or fine-tuning them; Hybrid Noise Initialization constrains global semantics, Weighted Window Sampling removes inter-window seams, and Temporal Extended Sampling builds long-range dependencies with timestep-varying fusion; Skip Residual Guidance extends it to enhancement; on VBench-Long it balances coherence and motion diversity better than FreeNoise, FreeLong and RIFLEx across two base models.
What the authors say about limits: an inference-time technique that adds no model knowledge; window and fusion hyperparameters depend on the backbone; VBench-Long is automatic and lacks human preference; the maximum length is not stated; no latency or VRAM reporting.
What it does not claim: a new long-video training method; that every backbone benefits equally; that it beats purpose-trained long-video models.
