规模律Scaling laws核验Checked 2026-11-17

Switch Transformer：用简单稀疏路由扩到万亿参数Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity

问题：MoE 参数多、算力恒定，但路由复杂、通信贵、训练不稳，所以一直没普及；能不能把路由简化成「每个 token 只选一个专家」，再配一套训练技巧把它跑稳？
证据位置：Switch 层与 top-1 路由在 §2；容量因子与负载均衡损失在 §2.2；训练与稳定性技巧（选择性精度、初始化、正则）在 §3；结果在 §4–§5，包含 T5-Base/Large 同算力 7× 预训练加速、101 种语言的 mT5 增益、万亿参数 Switch-C 对 T5-XXL 的 4× 加速；结论在 §9。
作者自陈局限：论文没有独立 Limitations 章节。Appendix E 明确提醒：上游困惑度不一定转化为下游任务——在最大模型区间（T5-11B/T5-XXL），Switch 模型并不总能把预训练优势带到 SuperGLUE 微调；作者说这需要未来研究，并指出专家模型的微调动态很复杂，依赖正则、负载均衡与超参。
它没主张的：没有说 Switch 在所有下游任务上都优于 dense——Appendix E 反而说小中模型区间两者相似，最大模型在 SuperGLUE 上不一定更好；没有说 top-1 路由普适最优——只与 GShard 等少数路由做了比较；也没有说 7× 加速能直接换算成 7× 成本优势——通信与专家并行开销未计入这句口号。Problem: MoE has many parameters at constant compute, but complex routing, expensive communication and unstable training kept it niche; can routing be simplified to one expert per token and paired with training tricks to make it stable?
Where the evidence sits: the Switch layer and top-1 routing in Section 2, capacity factor and load-balancing loss in 2.2, training and stability techniques (selective precision, initialization, regularization) in Section 3; results in Sections 4-5, including 7x pretraining speedup at equal compute for T5-Base/Large, gains across 101 languages for mT5, and a 4x speedup of trillion-parameter Switch-C over T5-XXL; conclusion in Section 9.
Stated limits: no separate Limitations section. Appendix E warns that upstream perplexity does not necessarily transfer downstream — in the largest regime (T5-11B/T5-XXL) Switch models do not always carry their pretraining advantage into SuperGLUE fine-tuning, which the authors leave to future work and attribute to complex fine-tuning dynamics depending on regularization, load balancing and hyperparameters.
What it does not claim: it does not claim Switch beats dense models on every downstream task — Appendix E says the two are similar in small-to-medium regimes and the largest Switch model is not always better on SuperGLUE; it does not claim top-1 routing is universally optimal, comparing only a few alternatives such as GShard; and it does not claim the 7x speedup translates directly into 7x cost savings, since communication and expert-parallel overhead are outside that headline.
