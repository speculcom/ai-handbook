Data Darwinism Part II: DataEvolve — AI can Autonomously Evolve Pretraining Data CurationData Darwinism Part II: DataEvolve — AI can Autonomously Evolve Pretraining Data Curation

问题：数据达尔文主义 Part I 给出十级处理阶梯，但策略仍靠人工为单一类目设计；现代语料有数百个异质类目，人工设计不可行。
证据位置：摘要给出闭环进化：识别质量问题→生成候选策略→在采样数据上执行→评估→跨代改进，并用问题池/策略池积累知识；在 Nemotron-CC 的 8 个类目、672B token 上跑 30 轮，产出 504B token 的 Darwin-CC；3B 模型训 500B token，18 个基准均分 44.13，优于 DCLM、Ultra-FineWeb、FineWeb-Edu，比原始数据 +3.96。
作者自陈局限：只在 Nemotron-CC 的 8 类上验证；3B/500B token 规模；进化出的策略集中在清洗（定向去噪、格式归一），不代表覆盖所有数据价值维度；30 轮迭代的算力成本未细报；没有更大模型验证。
它没主张的：不是“AI 自动造数据”；不是任何语料都能套用；不是 44.13 分可跨基准直接比较。Problem: Data Darwinism Part I gave a ten-level processing ladder but still required manual strategy design for a single category; modern corpora have hundreds of heterogeneous categories, making manual design prohibitive.
Where the evidence sits: a closed evolutionary loop identifies quality issues, generates candidate strategies, executes on sampled data, evaluates and refines across generations, accumulating an issue pool and a strategy pool; on 8 Nemotron-CC categories and 672B tokens it runs 30 iterations per category to produce the 504B-token Darwin-CC; 3B models trained on 500B tokens average 44.13 across 18 benchmarks, beating DCLM, Ultra-FineWeb and FineWeb-Edu and gaining 3.96 over raw data.
What the authors say about limits: validated only on 8 Nemotron-CC categories; 3B/500B-token scale; evolved strategies converge on cleaning (targeted denoising, format normalisation) and do not cover every dimension of data value; the compute cost of 30 iterations is not broken out; no larger-model validation.
What it does not claim: that AI generates data; that any corpus can use the same loop; that 44.13 is comparable across benchmark suites.
