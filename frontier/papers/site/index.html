Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback / Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

问题：训练无害助手通常要成千上万条人工无害性标注；能不能只给一小组自然语言原则，让模型自己批评并改写自己的回答，再用模型产生的偏好标签做强化学习。
证据位置：流程分两阶段——监督阶段（自我批评与改写，SL-CAI）在 §3，RLAIF 阶段在 §4；主要结果是 Figure 2 的 harmlessness–helpfulness Elo 散点（52B 的各次 RL run，图注明确只有差值有意义），图注原话是「The RL-CAI models trained with AI feedback learn to be less harmful at a given level of helpfulness.」；Elo 对比与 CoT 的取舍在 §4.3，回避性与标注指令变化在 §4.4，绝对有害度分数在 §4.5；讨论与未来方向在 §6、§6.1，双用风险在 §6.2。没有独立的 Limitations 章节，§6 起就是局限与未来方向的讨论。
作者自陈局限：§6 明说虽然去掉了无害性的人工反馈标签，本文仍然依赖人工监督形式的有用性标签，完全不用人工反馈就实现有用性与指令跟随留待未来工作；§1.1 脚注 2 承认这些原则是为研究目的以相当临时、迭代的方式挑出来的，未来应由更多利益相关方重新制定并按部署用途与地区调整；§4.3 报告 RL-CAI 会被过度训练而出现 Goodhart 行为——对有害提示反应过激，或在大多数红队提示后加套话；§4.5 提醒绝对有害度分数可能校准不好，因为不同标注者对 0–4 分有各自偏好；§6.1 把 robustness（能否让模型基本免疫红队攻击）列为仍未解决的问题；§6.2 指出降低人工反馈需求也让人更容易部署未经充分人工测试与观察的系统，而且方法本身是双用的。
它没主张的：论文没有说实现了完全无人类监督的对齐——有用性标签仍来自人，作者也写明目标不是彻底去掉人类监督而是让它更高效、更透明、更有针对性；也没有说 CAI 模型在所有维度上优于 RLHF，§4.3 写带 CoT 的 RL-CAI 看起来有用性略低、无害性略高；Elo 是相对分且图注声明只有差值有意义，§4.4 还说明本文改了标注指令（同样无害时要求选更不回避的回答）并换了标注供应商，因此与先前工作的 Elo 不能直接对比；「免疫红队攻击」是未来方向而不是本文结果。Problem: training a harmless assistant normally needs tens of thousands of human harmlessness labels; can a small set of natural language principles be enough, with the model critiquing and revising its own responses and a preference model trained on AI-generated labels driving the RL stage.
Where the evidence sits: the pipeline has two stages — the supervised stage (self-critique and revision, SL-CAI) in Section 3 and the RLAIF stage in Section 4; the main result is the harmlessness-versus-helpfulness Elo scatter in Figure 2 (all 52B RL runs, with the caption stating that only differences are meaningful), whose caption reads "The RL-CAI models trained with AI feedback learn to be less harmful at a given level of helpfulness."; Elo comparisons and the chain-of-thought tradeoff are in 4.3, evasiveness and the change of labeling instructions in 4.4, and absolute harmfulness scores in 4.5; discussion and future directions are in Section 6 and 6.1, and dual-use risk in 6.2. There is no separate Limitations section; Section 6 onward is the discussion of limits and future directions.
What the authors say about limits: Section 6 states that although human feedback labels for harmlessness were removed, this work still relied on human supervision in the form of helpfulness labels, and achieving helpfulness and instruction-following without human feedback is left to future work; footnote 2 in 1.1 acknowledges the principles were chosen in a fairly ad hoc and iterative way for research purposes and should be redeveloped by a larger set of stakeholders and adapted to the intended usage and deployment location; 4.3 reports that RL-CAI models can be over-trained, producing Goodharting behaviour such as overly harsh responses to harmful prompts or boilerplate language after most red-team prompts; 4.5 cautions that absolute harmfulness scores may not be well calibrated because different workers have personal biases about the 0-4 scale; 6.1 lists robustness — whether models can be made essentially immune to red-team attacks — as a remaining issue; and 6.2 notes that reducing the need for human feedback also makes it easier to deploy systems that have not been thoroughly tested and observed by humans, and that the methods are dual use.
What it does not claim: the paper does not claim alignment without any human supervision — helpfulness labels still come from humans, and the authors write that the goal is not to remove human supervision entirely but to make it more efficient, transparent and targeted; it does not claim CAI models are better than RLHF on every axis, and 4.3 says RL-CAI with CoT seems slightly less helpful but slightly more harmless than without CoT; Elo is a relative score whose caption says only differences are meaningful, and 4.4 explains that the labeling instructions were changed (prefer the less evasive response when both are equally harmless) and the vendor changed, so these Elo scores are not directly comparable with prior work; and immunity to red-team attacks is a future direction, not a result of this paper.

Language Models are Few-Shot Learners / Language Models are Few-Shot Learners

问题：把模型放大之后，不更新任何梯度、只在输入里给几个示例，能不能把任务做到接近专门微调的水平。
证据位置：摘要给出 175B 与「无梯度更新、无微调」的设定；九类任务的结果在 §3（LAMBADA 在 Figure 3.2 与表 3.2，开放域问答在表 3.3，SuperGLUE 在表 3.8，阅读理解在表 3.7，算术在 §3.9.1 与表 3.9）；训练集与测试集重叠的分析在 §4 与 Figure 4.2；局限有独立的 §5 Limitations，社会影响在 §6。
作者自陈局限：§5 写了多层——长文本生成仍会语义重复、在足够长的段落上失去连贯、自相矛盾；在 WIC、ANLI 这类「比较」任务以及部分阅读理解上，单样本甚至少样本几乎只比随机好一点点；只研究自回归模型，实验不含双向架构或去噪目标，作者承认这可能在需要双向性的任务上吃亏，并猜想同规模双向模型微调会更强；预训练目标对每个 token 等权、可能撞上目标函数本身的限制，模型也不接地于视频或物理交互；预训练样本效率仍远低于人类；这个规模的模型推理昂贵且不便于实际部署；决策不易解释、在新输入上校准不好、保留训练数据中的偏见。§5 还专门写了一条不确定性：少样本究竟是在推理时从零学会新任务，还是只是识别出训练中已学过的任务，作者说不清楚，而且可能因任务而异。§4 说明去污染只完成了一部分——一个 bug 导致检测到的重叠只删掉一部分，因训练成本无法重训，只能事后用 13-gram 重叠构造 clean 子集复测，PIQA 与 Winograd 被标记为可能受污染并在 §3 加星号。
它没主张的：摘要的原话是「有时甚至能与先前微调方法竞争」，不是在所有任务上超过微调；论文没有证明少样本等于在推理时学习新任务，§5 把这一点列为未解的歧义；也没有说训练数据与测试集完全隔离；实验只覆盖自回归语言模型，不能外推到双向架构或其他训练目标；§3 同时报告了 ANLI、RACE、QuAC 等少样本仍吃力的任务。Problem: after scaling a model up, can tasks be performed close to the level of task-specific fine-tuning with no gradient updates and only a few examples placed in the input.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract gives the 175B scale and the no-gradient-update, no-fine-tuning setting; results across nine task categories are in Section 3 (LAMBADA in Figure 3.2 and Table 3.2, open-domain QA in Table 3.3, SuperGLUE in Table 3.8, reading comprehension in Table 3.7, arithmetic in 3.9.1 and Table 3.9); train-test overlap analysis is in Section 4 and Figure 4.2; the limits have a dedicated Section 5 Limitations, and societal impact is in Section 6.
What the authors say about limits: Section 5 lists several layers — long-form synthesis still repeats itself semantically, loses coherence over sufficiently long passages and contradicts itself; on comparison tasks such as WIC and ANLI and on a subset of reading comprehension tasks, one-shot and even few-shot performance does little better than chance; only autoregressive models were studied, so the experiments include no bidirectional architectures or denoising objectives, which the authors acknowledge may cost performance on tasks that benefit from bidirectionality, and they conjecture a large bidirectional model would fine-tune better; the pretraining objective weights every token equally and may hit the limits of the objective itself, and the model is not grounded in video or physical interaction; pretraining sample efficiency remains far below a human's; models at this scale are expensive and inconvenient to run inference on; and decisions are not easily interpretable, not necessarily well calibrated on novel inputs, and retain the biases of the training data. Section 5 also states an explicit uncertainty: whether few-shot learning actually learns new tasks from scratch at inference time or simply recognises tasks learned during training is unclear and may vary by task. Section 4 explains that decontamination was only partial — a bug meant only part of the detected overlap was removed, retraining was not feasible given the cost, so clean subsets were built afterwards using 13-gram overlap and re-evaluated, with PIQA and Winograd flagged as possibly contaminated and asterisked in Section 3.
What it does not claim: the abstract says sometimes even reaching competitiveness with prior fine-tuning approaches, not beating fine-tuning on every task; the paper does not show that few-shot prompting equals learning a new task at inference time, and Section 5 leaves that as an unresolved ambiguity; it does not claim training and test data were fully separated; the experiments cover autoregressive language models only and cannot be extended to bidirectional architectures or other training objectives; and Section 3 also reports tasks where few-shot learning still struggles, including ANLI, RACE and QuAC.

FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness / FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness

问题：自注意力的时间与显存随序列长度平方增长，瓶颈不在算力而在显存读写；能不能不把 N×N 注意力矩阵写回显存也算出精确结果。
证据位置：摘要给出三档端到端加速与 7.6 倍注意力计算加速；局限写在第 5 节 Limitations and Future Directions。
作者自陈局限：三点——每换一种注意力实现就要重写一个 CUDA kernel，要用比 PyTorch 低得多的语言写、工程量大；实现未必能跨 GPU 架构移植；他们的 IO 优化只在单卡上达到常数级最优，而注意力计算本身是可跨多卡并行的。
它没主张的：论文没有说这是近似注意力——结果精确；但也没有声称解决了多卡切分问题，更没有说端到端加速比等于注意力算子加速比（摘要里 15% / 3× / 2.4× 与算子级 7.6× 是两个口径，不能混用）。Problem: self-attention time and memory grow quadratically with sequence length, and the bottleneck is memory traffic rather than arithmetic; can exact attention be computed without writing the N×N matrix back to HBM.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract gives three end-to-end speed-ups plus a 7.6× speed-up on the attention computation itself; the limits are in Section 5, Limitations and Future Directions.
What the authors say about limits: three — each new attention implementation needs a new CUDA kernel written in a much lower-level language than PyTorch, which is significant engineering effort; implementations may not transfer across GPU architectures; and their IO-aware version is optimal within constants on a single GPU, whereas the attention computation itself may be parallelisable across multiple GPUs.
What it does not claim: the paper does not present an approximation — the result is exact — but it also does not solve multi-GPU partitioning, and it does not equate end-to-end speed-up with kernel speed-up (the 15% / 3× / 2.4× figures and the 7.6× kernel figure are different measurements and must not be mixed).

QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs / QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs

问题：§1 指出对 LLaMA 65B 做常规 16-bit 微调需要 780GB 以上显存，单卡放不下；能不能把基座量化到 4-bit、只训练低秩适配器，同时把任务表现保住。
证据位置：方法在 §3（4-bit NormalFloat、Double Quantization、Paged Optimizers）；与全量微调的对照在 §4——表 3 是 RoBERTa 与 T5 在 125M–3B 上的 GLUE 与 Super-NaturalInstructions，表 4 是 LLaMA 7B–65B 的 5-shot MMLU；聊天机器人评测在 §5（表 5 为 MMLU，表 6 为 Vicuna 上相对 ChatGPT 得分的百分比、由 GPT-4 评判，表 7 为人工与 GPT-4 的 Elo）；局限有独立的 §8 Limitations and Discussion，评测方法的反思在 §6.2。
作者自陈局限：§8 第一段就写明——虽然有证据表明 4-bit 基座加 LoRA 能复现 16-bit 全量微调，但他们没有确立 QLoRA 在 33B 与 65B 规模上能追平 16-bit 全量微调，因资源成本太高留待未来工作；评测只覆盖 MMLU、Vicuna、OA，没测 BigBench、RAFT、HELM，不能保证结论外推到这些基准；只做了有限的负责任 AI 评测（表 8 的 CrowS 偏见），其他类型偏见是否同样表现未知；也没试 3-bit 等其他位宽或 LoRA 之外的适配器方法。摘要自己还写了当前聊天机器人基准不足以准确评估聊天机器人水平，§6.2 给出具体原因：人工标注一致性只有 Fleiss κ=0.42，GPT-4 有明显顺序效应、给自己的输出打分偏高（Elo 1348 对人工 1176），与人工的样本级一致性 κ=0.25。
它没主张的：99.3% 是 Vicuna 基准上相对 ChatGPT 得分的百分比、由 GPT-4 判定（表 6 口径），不是人类评测认定的等价，也不是在所有基准上追平；论文没有说 4-bit 训练在 33B/65B 上与全量微调无损等价，§8 明确否认这一点已经确立；Guanaco 只用交叉熵监督微调、没有用 RLHF（§6.2），所以这不是「4-bit 就能做出 ChatGPT 级助手」的完整配方；§4 的等价结论建立在学术基准与既有评测设置上，作者也提醒基准成绩很大程度取决于微调数据与基准数据的相似度。Problem: Section 1 notes that regular 16-bit finetuning of a LLaMA 65B model requires more than 780 GB of GPU memory, which does not fit on one card; can the base model be quantised to 4-bit and only low-rank adapters trained while keeping task performance.
Where the evidence sits: the method is in Section 3 (4-bit NormalFloat, Double Quantization, Paged Optimizers); the comparison against full finetuning is in Section 4 — Table 3 covers RoBERTa and T5 from 125M to 3B on GLUE and Super-NaturalInstructions, Table 4 covers LLaMA 7B to 65B on 5-shot MMLU; chatbot evaluation is in Section 5 (Table 5 MMLU, Table 6 Vicuna scores as a percentage of ChatGPT's score judged by GPT-4, Table 7 human and GPT-4 Elo); the limits have a dedicated Section 8 Limitations and Discussion, and reflection on evaluation methodology is in 6.2.
What the authors say about limits: the first paragraph of Section 8 states that despite the evidence that a 4-bit base with LoRA replicates 16-bit full finetuning, they did not establish that QLoRA can match full 16-bit finetuning at the 33B and 65B scales, and leave that to future work because of the immense resource costs; evaluation covers only MMLU, the Vicuna benchmark and the OA benchmark, not BigBench, RAFT or HELM, so generalisation to those is not ensured; only a limited responsible AI evaluation was done (CrowS bias in Table 8) and other bias types are unknown; and other bit-precisions such as 3-bit, or adapter methods other than LoRA, were not evaluated. The abstract itself says current chatbot benchmarks are not trustworthy for accurately evaluating chatbot performance levels, and 6.2 gives the reasons: human annotator agreement is only Fleiss kappa 0.42, GPT-4 shows strong order effects and rates its own outputs higher (Elo 1348 versus 1176 from humans), and sample-level agreement with humans is kappa 0.25.
What it does not claim: the 99.3% figure is a percentage of ChatGPT's score on the Vicuna benchmark as judged by GPT-4 (the Table 6 measure), not human-certified equivalence and not parity across all benchmarks; the paper does not claim lossless equivalence to full finetuning at 33B/65B, and Section 8 explicitly denies that this was established; Guanaco was trained with cross-entropy supervised finetuning only and no RLHF (6.2), so this is not a complete recipe for a ChatGPT-level assistant on 4-bit; and the equivalence result in Section 4 rests on academic benchmarks with established evaluation setups, with the authors cautioning that benchmark scores depend heavily on how similar the finetuning data is to the benchmark data.

GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers / GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers

问题：几百亿参数的模型光显存就装不下，训练后量化到低比特往往掉点严重；能不能在不重训的前提下压到 3–4 bit 还基本不掉。
证据位置：175B 模型约 4 个 GPU 小时压到 3 或 4 bit 的结论在摘要；局限同时写在引言贡献段与第 6 节 Summary and Limitations。
作者自陈局限：两点很关键——加速来自减少显存搬运，不是减少计算量，主流硬件不支持 FP16×INT4 这类混合精度乘加，因此实际矩阵乘法并不会变快；研究只覆盖生成任务，未做激活量化（作者认为激活不是目标场景的瓶颈）。
它没主张的：论文没有说量化后推理整体更快，也没有覆盖激活量化与更大的压缩率；「单卡跑 175B」指的是权重能装进一张卡，不等于该配置的吞吐满足线上服务。Problem: models with tens of billions of parameters do not fit in memory, and post-training quantisation to low bit-width usually costs accuracy; can 3-4 bits be reached without retraining and with almost no loss.
Where the evidence sits: the 175B-in-four-GPU-hours result at 3 or 4 bits is in the abstract; the limits appear both in the introduction’s contribution list and in Section 6, Summary and Limitations.
What the authors say about limits: two matter — the speed-up comes from reduced memory movement, not from fewer computations, and mainstream hardware lacks support for mixed-precision operands such as FP16 by INT4, so the actual multiplications are not faster; and the study covers generative tasks only and does not quantise activations, which the authors consider not a bottleneck in their target setting.
What it does not claim: the paper does not claim faster end-to-end inference, nor cover activation quantisation or higher compression; "175B on a single GPU" means the weights fit on one card, not that throughput meets serving requirements.

Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces / Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces

问题：此前的次二次方架构（线性注意力、门控卷积、结构化状态空间模型）在语言这类离散模态上不如注意力，作者判断关键弱点是不能按内容决定记住或忘掉什么；能不能让 SSM 的参数随输入变化，同时还能高效训练。
证据位置：选择机制在 §3.2，硬件感知的并行扫描在 §3.3，去掉注意力与 MLP 块的简化架构在 §3.4；语言建模的 scaling law 在 §4.2.1 与 Figure 6（约 125M 到 1.3B，Pile 数据，按 Chinchilla 协议），零样本下游在表 1，速度与显存在 §4.5 与 Figure 12；局限写在 §5 Discussion 的 No Free Lunch、Downstream Affordances、Scaling 三段。

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models / Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

问题：语言模型的交叉熵损失随参数量、数据量、训练算力怎么变，固定算力预算又该怎么分配，能不能写成可外推的公式。
证据位置：三条基本幂律在 §1.2 与 Figure 1（α_N≈0.076、α_D≈0.095、α_Cmin≈0.050），过拟合与数据/参数比例在 §4，算力最优分配在 §6.1 与 Figure 14（N(C_min)∝C_min^0.73，数据需求约按 C^0.27 增长）；局限有专门的 Appendix C Caveats，另有 §6.3 Contradictions and a Conjecture 与 §8 Discussion。
作者自陈局限：Appendix C 列了六条——目前对提出的任何标度律都没有扎实的理论理解，缺了理论或对修正项的系统认识就难以判断它们在什么情况下可信；对远超实验损失范围的 B_crit(L) 预测没有信心；小数据区没有仔细研究，最小 D 上 L(N,D) 拟合很差，也没试正则化与数据增强；算力用 C≈6NBS 估计、未计入与上下文长度成正比的项，因此在 n_ctx 很大时算力标度可能被混淆；可能漏调了某些影响标度的超参（如初始化尺度或动量）；学习率最优值依赖目标损失，未跑到收敛的短训练没有试过更高学习率。§6.3 还自己指出：外推到远高于实验的规模时 L(C_min) 与 L(D) 会相交而出现矛盾，说明幂律在那之前必然失效，交点是否代表性能上限只是作者的猜想。
它没主张的：论文测的是交叉熵损失，不是下游任务成绩——§8 明确把「损失继续下降是否转化为相关语言任务的提升」列为待研究；也没有说这些关系适用于语言以外，§8 写作者尚不知道哪些结果依赖自然语言数据的结构、哪些是普适的；「大模型配相对少量数据并显著早停」是给定算力预算下的分配结论，不是「数据不重要」的普遍主张，作者同时写明趋势最终必然变平，因为自然语言的熵非零。Problem: how does language model cross-entropy loss change with parameter count, dataset size and training compute, how should a fixed compute budget be allocated, and can this be written as extrapolatable equations.
Where the evidence sits: the three basic power laws are in 1.2 and Figure 1 (alpha_N about 0.076, alpha_D about 0.095, alpha_Cmin about 0.050), overfitting and the data-to-parameter ratio in Section 4, and optimal compute allocation in 6.1 and Figure 14 (N(C_min) proportional to C_min^0.73, with data requirements growing roughly as C^0.27); the limits have a dedicated Appendix C Caveats, plus 6.3 Contradictions and a Conjecture and Section 8 Discussion.
What the authors say about limits: Appendix C lists six — there is at present no solid theoretical understanding of any of the proposed scaling laws, and without a theory or a systematic understanding of the corrections it is difficult to determine when they can be trusted; they are not especially confident in the B_crit(L) prediction far outside the explored loss range; the small data regime was not thoroughly investigated, fits for L(N,D) were poor at the smallest D, and regularisation and data augmentation were not tried; compute was estimated as C about 6NBS without the term proportional to context length, so compute scalings may be confounded at very large n_ctx; some hyperparameter affecting scaling (such as initialisation scale or momentum) may have been left untuned; and the optimal learning rate depends on the target loss, with higher rates not tried for runs that did not reach convergence. Section 6.3 also notes that extrapolating far beyond the studied scale makes L(C_min) and L(D) intersect, an apparent contradiction implying the laws must break down before that point, and whether the intersection marks maximal performance is offered only as a conjecture.
What it does not claim: the paper measures cross-entropy loss, not downstream task scores — Section 8 explicitly leaves open whether continued loss improvement translates into improvement on relevant language tasks; it does not claim the relations hold outside language, and Section 8 says the authors do not know which results depend on the structure of natural language data and which are universal; and very large models on relatively modest data with early stopping is an allocation result under a fixed compute budget, not a general claim that data does not matter — the authors also write that the trends must eventually level off because natural language has non-zero entropy.

Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 74% success for ToT vs 4% for GPT-4 with chain-of-thought prompting on Game of 24; the experiment is in Section 4.1, Table 2 — the relatively hard games indexed 901-1,000 out of 1,362 scraped from 4nums.com, success rate over 100 games, GPT-4 at temperature 0.7 (experiments run May 5-16, 2023); cost and limits are in Section 6, Discussion, and Appendix B.3.
What the authors say about limits: Section 6 states that deliberate search "might not be necessary for many existing tasks that GPT-4 already excels at" and that, as an initial step, only three relatively simple tasks are explored; Appendix B.3 states ToT "could require 5-100 times more generated tokens than CoT"; the work uses an off-the-shelf LM, with fine-tuning left as a future direction.
What it does not claim: 74% is the success rate on the 100-game hard subset with BFS keeping b=5 candidates per step, not a result on all 1,362 games; the paper does not show ToT beats CoT everywhere — Appendix B.1 reports only slight gains on GSM8K/StrategyQA and Appendix B.2 reports GPT-3.5+ToT at 19% on Game of 24; the authors note general crosswords can be solved by specialized NLP pipelines, and no training-level claim is made.
