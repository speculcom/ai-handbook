Abstain-R1: Calibrated Abstention and Post-Refusal Clarification via Verifiable RL

问题：模型要么硬答、要么一句「无法回答」结束；缺的是「该弃答时弃答，弃答后还能问清缺什么」的可校准行为。
证据位置：摘要报告一个 3B 模型，用 clarification-aware RLVR 奖励，在不可答查询上同时改善弃答与澄清，并保持可答查询上的表现；方法属于可验证奖励的强化学习，不是提示词模板。
作者自陈局限：3B 规模，不能直接外推到 70B+；训练/评测任务以不可答查询为主，开放域闲聊与多轮澄清覆盖有限；奖励设计依赖可验证的答案/澄清标签，标签本身有噪声；没有生产部署验证。
它没主张的：不是通用助手模型；不是解决所有幻觉；不是「弃答越多越好」；不是不需要人工评估的自动对齐方案。Problem: models either answer anyway or stop with “cannot answer”; what is missing is calibratable behaviour that abstains when appropriate and then asks for the missing information.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a 3B model trained with a clarification-aware RLVR reward that improves abstention and clarification on unanswerable queries while preserving strong performance on answerable ones; it is verifiable-reward RL, not a prompt template.
What the authors say about limits: 3B scale, not directly extrapolable to 70B+; training and evaluation centre on unanswerable queries, with limited coverage of open-domain chat and multi-turn clarification; the reward depends on verifiable answer/clarification labels, which carry noise; no production deployment is reported.
What it does not claim: a general assistant model; a solution to all hallucinations; that more abstention is always better; an auto-alignment method that needs no human evaluation.

AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents

问题：LLM 作为 agent 在不同交互环境里的能力，缺一个统一、可比较的量尺。
证据位置：摘要写明 8 个环境，测推理与决策；商业模型与 ≤70B 开源模型差距明显；失败主因是长程推理、决策与指令跟随。
作者自陈局限：环境与任务是固定的文本交互，不是真实业务；商业 API 与开源模型的推理预算未统一；代码训练对不同 agent 任务影响矛盾，作者未给出统一因果。
它没主张的：不是真实业务代理排行榜；不是单一能力总分；不证明某个模型在所有环境都更强。Problem: there was no common yardstick for LLM-as-agent ability across interactive environments.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract states 8 distinct environments measuring reasoning and decision-making; it reports a significant gap between commercial models and OSS models up to 70B; the main failures are long-horizon reasoning, decision-making and instruction following.
What the authors say about limits: environments are fixed text interactions, not real business; inference budgets are not equalised between commercial APIs and OSS models; the effect of code training is ambivalent across agent tasks and no unified causal explanation is given.
What it does not claim: not a real-business agent leaderboard; not a single ability score; not proof that one model wins in every environment.

AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization for LLM Compression and Acceleration

问题：LLM 低比特权重量化如何保精度、又保持硬件友好，能落到端侧。
证据位置：摘要称只保护 1% 显著权重即可大幅降低量化误差；显著通道要按激活分布而非权重判断；用等价缩放保护显著通道；不依赖反向传播或重建；TinyChat 在桌面/移动 GPU 上相对 HF FP16 有 3× 以上加速；可在移动 GPU 跑 70B Llama-2。
作者自陈局限：权重量化，激活仍较高精度；校准集有偏；3× 是特定实现/硬件；未覆盖所有模型结构。
它没主张的：不是训练方法；不是 4-bit 无损；不是所有设备都能跑 70B。Problem: how to quantise LLM weights to low bits while keeping accuracy and hardware-friendly deployment on edge devices?
Where the evidence sits: the abstract says protecting only 1% salient weights greatly reduces quantisation error; saliency should be judged from activations, not weights; an equivalent scaling protects salient channels; no backprop or reconstruction is used; TinyChat reports >3x speedup over HF FP16 on desktop and mobile GPUs and runs 70B Llama-2 on mobile GPUs.
What the authors say about limits: weight-only quantisation, activations stay higher precision; calibration set bias; 3x is implementation/hardware-specific; not every model architecture is covered.
What it does not claim: not a training method; not lossless 4-bit; not every device can run 70B.

Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

问题：训练无害助手通常要成千上万条人工无害性标注；能不能只给一小组自然语言原则，让模型自己批评并改写自己的回答，再用模型产生的偏好标签做强化学习。
证据位置：流程分两阶段——监督阶段（自我批评与改写，SL-CAI）在 §3，RLAIF 阶段在 §4；主要结果是 Figure 2 的 harmlessness–helpfulness Elo 散点（52B 的各次 RL run，图注明确只有差值有意义），图注原话是「The RL-CAI models trained with AI feedback learn to be less harmful at a given level of helpfulness.」；Elo 对比与 CoT 的取舍在 §4.3，回避性与标注指令变化在 §4.4，绝对有害度分数在 §4.5；讨论与未来方向在 §6、§6.1，双用风险在 §6.2。没有独立的 Limitations 章节，§6 起就是局限与未来方向的讨论。
作者自陈局限：§6 明说虽然去掉了无害性的人工反馈标签，本文仍然依赖人工监督形式的有用性标签，完全不用人工反馈就实现有用性与指令跟随留待未来工作；§1.1 脚注 2 承认这些原则是为研究目的以相当临时、迭代的方式挑出来的，未来应由更多利益相关方重新制定并按部署用途与地区调整；§4.3 报告 RL-CAI 会被过度训练而出现 Goodhart 行为——对有害提示反应过激，或在大多数红队提示后加套话；§4.5 提醒绝对有害度分数可能校准不好，因为不同标注者对 0–4 分有各自偏好；§6.1 把 robustness（能否让模型基本免疫红队攻击）列为仍未解决的问题；§6.2 指出降低人工反馈需求也让人更容易部署未经充分人工测试与观察的系统，而且方法本身是双用的。
它没主张的：论文没有说实现了完全无人类监督的对齐——有用性标签仍来自人，作者也写明目标不是彻底去掉人类监督而是让它更高效、更透明、更有针对性；也没有说 CAI 模型在所有维度上优于 RLHF，§4.3 写带 CoT 的 RL-CAI 看起来有用性略低、无害性略高；Elo 是相对分且图注声明只有差值有意义，§4.4 还说明本文改了标注指令（同样无害时要求选更不回避的回答）并换了标注供应商，因此与先前工作的 Elo 不能直接对比；「免疫红队攻击」是未来方向而不是本文结果。Problem: training a harmless assistant normally needs tens of thousands of human harmlessness labels; can a small set of natural language principles be enough, with the model critiquing and revising its own responses and a preference model trained on AI-generated labels driving the RL stage.
Where the evidence sits: the pipeline has two stages — the supervised stage (self-critique and revision, SL-CAI) in Section 3 and the RLAIF stage in Section 4; the main result is the harmlessness-versus-helpfulness Elo scatter in Figure 2 (all 52B RL runs, with the caption stating that only differences are meaningful), whose caption reads "The RL-CAI models trained with AI feedback learn to be less harmful at a given level of helpfulness."; Elo comparisons and the chain-of-thought tradeoff are in 4.3, evasiveness and the change of labeling instructions in 4.4, and absolute harmfulness scores in 4.5; discussion and future directions are in Section 6 and 6.1, and dual-use risk in 6.2. There is no separate Limitations section; Section 6 onward is the discussion of limits and future directions.
What the authors say about limits: Section 6 states that although human feedback labels for harmlessness were removed, this work still relied on human supervision in the form of helpfulness labels, and achieving helpfulness and instruction-following without human feedback is left to future work; footnote 2 in 1.1 acknowledges the principles were chosen in a fairly ad hoc and iterative way for research purposes and should be redeveloped by a larger set of stakeholders and adapted to the intended usage and deployment location; 4.3 reports that RL-CAI models can be over-trained, producing Goodharting behaviour such as overly harsh responses to harmful prompts or boilerplate language after most red-team prompts; 4.5 cautions that absolute harmfulness scores may not be well calibrated because different workers have personal biases about the 0-4 scale; 6.1 lists robustness — whether models can be made essentially immune to red-team attacks — as a remaining issue; and 6.2 notes that reducing the need for human feedback also makes it easier to deploy systems that have not been thoroughly tested and observed by humans, and that the methods are dual use.
What it does not claim: the paper does not claim alignment without any human supervision — helpfulness labels still come from humans, and the authors write that the goal is not to remove human supervision entirely but to make it more efficient, transparent and targeted; it does not claim CAI models are better than RLHF on every axis, and 4.3 says RL-CAI with CoT seems slightly less helpful but slightly more harmless than without CoT; Elo is a relative score whose caption says only differences are meaningful, and 4.4 explains that the labeling instructions were changed (prefer the less evasive response when both are equally harmless) and the vendor changed, so these Elo scores are not directly comparable with prior work; and immunity to red-team attacks is a future direction, not a result of this paper.

Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs

问题：不同模态的模型是否在收敛到同一个「柏拉图式」的表征空间？如果会，小模型能否用更好的训练视角追上大模型？
证据位置：摘要报告一个 109M 参数的文本 JEPA，在跨模态对齐上优于作者评测过的所有语言模型（最大到 72B）；同段给出「好视角比 next-token 少 42 倍样本」的结论。正文用 JEPA 作为可证明/可改进的实例，而不是只做相关性观察。
作者自陈局限：这是预印本；文本 JEPA 只有 109M，规模远小于被比较的 LLM；对齐指标衡量的是表征相似度，不是生成质量；42× 是特定视角与数据条件下的样本效率，不是通用常数；没有证明所有模态都收敛到同一空间。
它没主张的：不是新的 SOTA 语言模型；不是 next-token prediction 的替代品；不是「小模型全面超过大模型」；不是对 PRH 的最终证明。Problem: are models across modalities converging on a shared “Platonic” representation space? If so, can a small model catch up with a large one by using better training views?
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a 109M-parameter text JEPA that aligns with DINOv2 and CLIP image encoders better than every language model the authors evaluated, up to 72B; the same abstract states that good views need 42 times fewer training samples than next-token prediction. The body uses JEPA as a provable/improveable instance rather than a correlation-only observation.
What the authors say about limits: it is a preprint; the text JEPA is 109M, far smaller than the LLMs it is compared with; the alignment metric measures representation similarity, not generation quality; 42× is sample efficiency under specific views and data, not a universal constant; it does not prove every modality converges to one space.
What it does not claim: a new state-of-the-art language model; a replacement for next-token prediction; that small models beat large models across the board; a final proof of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis.

Bernini: Latent Semantic Planning for Video Diffusion

问题：多模态 LLM 擅长语义推理，扩散模型擅长像素保真，但两者各做各的；视频生成/编辑仍缺“先规划语义、再渲染像素”的分工。
证据位置：摘要提出 Bernini，MLLM planner 直接在 ViT embedding 空间预测目标语义表示，DiT renderer 以该计划、文本特征和（编辑时的）源 VAE 特征为条件合成像素；语义作为接口，planner 与 renderer 可分开训练、轻度联合；引入 SA-3D RoPE 处理多视觉输入，并在 planner 中加入 CoT；论文称在多项生成/编辑基准达 SOTA。
作者自陈局限：MLLM planner 与 DiT renderer 都很大，训练/推理成本高；语义接口的“可解释”不等于可控；SOTA 声明依赖其基准选择；编辑任务对源 VAE 特征依赖强；没有长视频/实时性报告。
它没主张的：不是通用视频编辑器；不是“语义计划一定比像素扩散好”；不是开源可商用（许可需核）。Problem: multimodal LLMs reason over semantics and diffusion models render pixels, but the two families remain separate; video generation and editing lack a division of labour between semantic planning and rendering.
Where the evidence sits: Bernini’s MLLM planner predicts the target semantic representation directly in ViT embedding space, and a DiT renderer synthesises pixels conditioned on that plan plus text features and, for editing, source VAE features; semantics act as the interface so planner and renderer train separately with light co-training; SA-3D RoPE handles multiple visual inputs and CoT is added to the planner; the paper reports state of the art on several generation and editing benchmarks.
What the authors say about limits: both planner and renderer are large, so training and inference are costly; an interpretable semantic interface is not the same as controllable output; SOTA depends on the chosen benchmarks; editing relies heavily on source VAE features; no long-video or real-time reporting.
What it does not claim: a general video editor; that semantic planning always beats pixel-space diffusion; that it is open-source or commercially usable without checking the licence.

The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All Large Language Models are in 1.58 Bits

问题：大模型权重能否从 16 位压到接近 1 位，同时不靠训练后量化补救，而是在预训练阶段就学会三值权重。
证据位置：摘要定义每个权重取 {-1, 0, 1}，称在同模型规模与同训练 token 下匹配 FP16/BF16 Transformer 的困惑度与任务表现；正文表 1 给 3B 规模下相对 LLaMA 快 2.71 倍、显存少 3.55 倍；3.9B 版本快 2.4 倍、显存少 3.32 倍且任务更好。
作者自陈局限：论文标注为 work in progress；主要验证在 3B 量级；4 位以下激活压缩留作未来工作；没有覆盖完整推理生态与所有下游任务。
它没主张的：不是所有模型都能无损三值化；不是训练后量化替代品；不是已经能在通用 GPU 上直接获得同等端到端加速。Problem: can LLM weights go from 16 bits to near 1 bit without relying on post-training quantization, by learning ternary weights during pre-training.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract defines every weight as {-1, 0, 1} and says it matches an FP16/BF16 Transformer of the same size and training tokens on perplexity and end-task performance; Table 1 reports a 3B model 2.71× faster and 3.55× less GPU memory than LLaMA; a 3.9B version is 2.4× faster, uses 3.32× less memory and performs better.
What the authors say about limits: the paper is marked work in progress; validation is mainly around 3B; compression of activations below 4 bits is left as future work; it does not cover the full inference ecosystem or every downstream task.
What it does not claim: not every model can be losslessly ternarized; not a replacement for post-training quantization; not an end-to-end speedup already available on every GPU stack.

BLIP-2: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training with Frozen Image Encoders and Large Language Models

问题：视觉语言预训练成本高，能否冻结图像编码器与大语言模型，只训练轻量桥接模块。
证据位置：摘要给出冻结图像编码器与冻结 LLM，用轻量 Querying Transformer 两阶段预训练；在 zero-shot VQAv2 上比 Flamingo80B 高 8.7%，可训练参数少 54×；支持零样本图生文指令。
作者自陈局限：冻结组件限制领域适配；两阶段训练仍有数据依赖；评测以 VQA/生成任务为主；未证明所有模态与语言。
它没主张的：不是无训练；不是取代全量微调；不是所有任务都优于大模型。Problem: vision-language pretraining is expensive; can we freeze the image encoder and the LLM and train only a lightweight bridge?
Where the evidence sits: the abstract uses frozen pretrained image encoders and frozen LLMs with a lightweight Querying Transformer trained in two stages; it reports +8.7% on zero-shot VQAv2 over Flamingo80B with 54x fewer trainable parameters and supports zero-shot image-to-text instructions.
What the authors say about limits: frozen components limit domain adaptation; two-stage training still depends on data; evaluation centres on VQA/generation; not all modalities or languages are covered.
What it does not claim: not training-free; not a replacement for full fine-tuning; not better than large models on every task.

规模律Scaling laws

Language Models are Few-Shot Learners

问题：把模型放大之后，不更新任何梯度、只在输入里给几个示例，能不能把任务做到接近专门微调的水平。
证据位置：摘要给出 175B 与「无梯度更新、无微调」的设定；九类任务的结果在 §3（LAMBADA 在 Figure 3.2 与表 3.2，开放域问答在表 3.3，SuperGLUE 在表 3.8，阅读理解在表 3.7，算术在 §3.9.1 与表 3.9）；训练集与测试集重叠的分析在 §4 与 Figure 4.2；局限有独立的 §5 Limitations，社会影响在 §6。

Recognition–Refusal Misalignment in LLMs

问题：模型明明「知道」一个问题不可答，为什么还是会编一个答案？是表征没编码「不可答」，还是编码了却没接到拒答行为上？
证据位置：论文在隐藏状态里找到一条线性方向，能把可答与「结构上不可能」的数学/代码题分开；识别方向与安全拒答方向接近正交，因此作者把问题解释为路由失败，而不是编码失败。
作者自陈局限：实验集中在数学与代码的结构性不可答题，不是所有「不知道」；线性探针能分开，不等于模型在生成时一定使用这条方向；拒答还受提示、语言与安全策略影响；论文没有给出通用修复方案。
它没主张的：不是所有幻觉都来自路由；不是「加一个拒答头」就能解决；不是对安全对齐的全面评估；不是跨语言、跨领域的普适结论。Problem: if a model represents that a question is unanswerable, why does it still invent an answer? Is the representation missing, or is it encoded but not routed to refusal?
Where the evidence sits: the paper finds a single linear direction in hidden states that separates answerable from structurally impossible math and code prompts; the recognition direction is close to orthogonal to the safety-refusal direction, so the authors explain the failure as routing rather than encoding.
What the authors say about limits: experiments focus on structurally unanswerable math and code, not every kind of “don’t know”; a linear probe separating the directions does not prove the model uses that direction when generating; refusal also depends on prompts, language and safety policy; the paper offers no general fix.
What it does not claim: that every hallucination is a routing failure; that adding a refusal head solves it; a comprehensive evaluation of safety alignment; a universal cross-language, cross-domain conclusion.

它没主张的：论文没有说思维链让模型获得了可靠推理能力，也没有说小模型加提示就能复现；作者明确写了它对 10B 以下多数模型反而有害，所以「加一句 let’s think step by step 就变聪明」不是这篇的结论。Problem: on complex reasoning tasks, answering directly works poorly; can the model be made to write intermediate steps before concluding.
Where the evidence sits: the scale-emergence result is Figure 4 and Section 3.2; the GSM8K numbers are Tables 1 and 2 (PaLM 540B from 17.9 to 56.9).
What the authors say about limits: Section 6 lists four — a chain of thought emulates human reasoning but does not answer whether the network is actually reasoning, which is left open; manually annotating chains is cheap in the few-shot setting but could be prohibitive for fine-tuning; there is no guarantee of correct reasoning paths, so wrong paths also produce plausible answers; and the ability emerges only at large scale, making real-world serving costly.
What it does not claim: the paper does not say chain of thought confers reliable reasoning, nor that small models can reproduce it with a prompt — the authors state it actually hurts most models under 10B parameters, so "just add let us think step by step" is not the finding here.

ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

问题：推理（思维链）与行动（动作规划）此前被分开研究，能不能让模型交替「想一步、做一步、看结果」再继续。
证据位置：摘要给出 ALFWorld 与 WebShop 分别高出模仿学习与强化学习 34% 与 10% 的绝对成功率；HotpotQA 27.4、FEVER 60.9 在表 1。
作者自陈局限：没有独立的 Limitations 章节，边界散在 §3.3 与 §6——交替结构提高了可追溯性，但也限制了推理自由度，推理错误率比纯思维链更高；检索到的信息是否有用是成败关键，无效检索占错误案例的 23%；大动作空间任务需要更多示例，很容易超出上下文长度上限；作者还指出所有提示方法都仍显著落后于领域专用的 SOTA，且离人类专家水平很远。
它没主张的：论文没有说 ReAct 在所有任务上都优于思维链——在 HotpotQA 上它比 CoT 略低（27.4 对 29.4），只在 FEVER 上更高；也没有说它能替代微调或达到人类水平。Problem: reasoning (chain of thought) and acting (action planning) had been studied separately; can a model interleave thinking, acting and observing.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract gives absolute success-rate gains of 34% and 10% over imitation and reinforcement learning on ALFWorld and WebShop; HotpotQA 27.4 and FEVER 60.9 are in Table 1.
What the authors say about limits: no separate Limitations section; the boundaries are in 3.3 and 6 — the interleaved structure improves groundedness and trustworthiness but constrains reasoning flexibility, raising the reasoning error rate above plain chain of thought; whether retrieval returns useful knowledge is critical, and non-informative search accounts for 23% of error cases; tasks with large action spaces need more demonstrations, which easily exceed the in-context length limit; and all prompting methods remain well behind domain-specific state of the art and far from expert humans.
What it does not claim: the paper does not say ReAct beats chain of thought on every task — on HotpotQA it is slightly lower (27.4 versus 29.4) and only higher on FEVER — nor that it replaces fine-tuning or reaches human level.

Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models

问题：LM 推理仍是 token 级、从左到右的决策，在需要探索、策略前瞻或初始决策起关键作用的任务上会失效；能否让 LM 在以「思维」为中间步骤的树上做 deliberate 搜索——考虑多条推理路径、自评估选择、必要时前瞻与回溯。
证据位置：摘要给出 Game of 24 上 GPT-4 + CoT 仅解出 4% 而 ToT 达 74%；实验在 Section 4.1 Table 2——从 4nums.com 抓取的 1,362 局中取较难的第 901-1,000 局、以 100 局成功率为指标，GPT-4、温度 0.7（实验于 2023 年 5 月 5-16 日进行）；成本与局限在 Section 6 Discussion 与 Appendix B.3。
作者自陈局限：Section 6 写明这类 deliberate search "might not be necessary for many existing tasks that GPT-4 already excels at"，且作为初步工作只探索了三个相对简单的任务；Appendix B.3 写明 "could require 5-100 times more generated tokens than CoT"；工作只用现成 LM，微调被留作未来方向。
它没主张的：74% 是 100 局较难子集、BFS 每步保留 b=5 候选设置下的成功率，不是全部 1,362 局的成绩；论文没有证明 ToT 处处优于 CoT——Appendix B.1 在 GSM8K/StrategyQA 上只有小幅提升，B.2 中 GPT-3.5+ToT 在 Game of 24 仅 19%；作者也指出一般填字游戏可由专用 NLP 管线解决，本文并未主张取代它们，更没有任何训练层面的结论。Problem: LM inference remains token-level, left-to-right decision making, which falls short on tasks requiring exploration, strategic lookahead, or where initial decisions play a pivotal role; can an LM instead search deliberately over a tree with thoughts as intermediate steps — considering multiple reasoning paths, self-evaluating choices, and looking ahead or backtracking when necessary.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 74% success for ToT vs 4% for GPT-4 with chain-of-thought prompting on Game of 24; the experiment is in Section 4.1, Table 2 — the relatively hard games indexed 901-1,000 out of 1,362 scraped from 4nums.com, success rate over 100 games, GPT-4 at temperature 0.7 (experiments run May 5-16, 2023); cost and limits are in Section 6, Discussion, and Appendix B.3.
What the authors say about limits: Section 6 states that deliberate search "might not be necessary for many existing tasks that GPT-4 already excels at" and that, as an initial step, only three relatively simple tasks are explored; Appendix B.3 states ToT "could require 5-100 times more generated tokens than CoT"; the work uses an off-the-shelf LM, with fine-tuning left as a future direction.
What it does not claim: 74% is the success rate on the 100-game hard subset with BFS keeping b=5 candidates per step, not a result on all 1,362 games; the paper does not show ToT beats CoT everywhere — Appendix B.1 reports only slight gains on GSM8K/StrategyQA and Appendix B.2 reports GPT-3.5+ToT at 19% on Game of 24; the authors note general crosswords can be solved by specialized NLP pipelines, and no training-level claim is made.

YaRN: Efficient Context Window Extension of Large Language Models

问题：RoPE 模型超出训练长度后失效，如何用更少 token 与训练步数扩展上下文窗口。
