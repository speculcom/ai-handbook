可解释性Interpretability

Locate, Steer, and Improve: A Practical Survey of Actionable Mechanistic Interpretability in Large Language ModelsLocate, Steer, and Improve: A Practical Survey of Actionable Mechanistic Interpretability in Large Language Models

问题：现有机制可解释性综述多把 MI 当观察科学，总结分析洞见，却缺一套可执行干预框架。
证据位置：摘要把综述结构化为“Locate（诊断）→ Steer（干预）→ Improve（优化）”，按可解释对象形式化分类定位与操控方法，并展示该框架如何在对齐、能力、效率上带来可度量改进；附带精选论文列表。
作者自陈局限：是综述，不是新实验；覆盖以 Transformer LLM 为主；干预效果依赖模型与任务，综述难以统一比较；论文列表有时效性；未系统讨论安全/滥用风险。
它没主张的：不是“MI 已能完全解释 LLM”；不是一套通用工具链；不是所有干预都能稳定提升。Problem: existing mechanistic-interpretability reviews treat MI as an observational science, summarising insights without a systematic framework for actionable intervention.
Where the evidence sits: the survey is structured as Locate (diagnosis) → Steer (intervention) → Improve (optimisation), formally categorises localising and steering methods by interpretable object, and shows how the framework enables measurable gains in alignment, capability and efficiency; a curated paper list accompanies it.
What the authors say about limits: a survey, not new experiments; coverage centres on Transformer LLMs; intervention effects depend on model and task and are hard to compare across a survey; the paper list ages; safety and misuse risks are not systematically discussed.
What it does not claim: that MI fully explains LLMs; a universal toolchain; that every intervention improves stably.
