适配Adaptation

ChainDoRA: Tensor-Train Factorized Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation for Parameter-Efficient LLM Fine-TuningChainDoRA: Tensor-Train Factorized Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation for Parameter-Efficient LLM Fine-Tuning

问题：LoRA 用两个低秩矩阵；DoRA 把权重拆成幅度与方向，但方向分支仍是稠密的 LoRA 式分解，参数成本没有真正降下来。
证据位置：摘要给出受控设置（15,119 条 response-only 数据、LLaMA-7B、7 个常识推理基准）：TT rank 16 的七任务平均 72.30%，LoRA 69.88%、DoRA 69.39%；可训练参数 5.35M，对比 LoRA 56.10M、DoRA 56.98M，较 DoRA 少 90.62%。
作者自陈局限：只测一个 7B 模型和一组 15,119 条适配数据；任务是常识推理，未覆盖指令跟随、多语言、长上下文；消融只动 TT rank 与适配位置；未报告生产部署。
它没主张的：不是所有 PEFT 场景的 SOTA；不是全面替代 DoRA；不是训练吞吐或显存的完整评测；不是跨模型规模可直接外推的结论。Problem: LoRA uses two low-rank matrices; DoRA separates weight magnitude and direction, but its directional branch keeps a dense LoRA-style factorization, so the parameter cost does not really come down.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a controlled setting (15,119 response-only examples, LLaMA-7B, seven commonsense-reasoning benchmarks): TT rank 16 reaches a seven-task average of 72.30% versus 69.88% for LoRA and 69.39% for DoRA, with 5.35M trainable parameters versus 56.10M and 56.98M, a 90.62% reduction against DoRA.
What the authors say about limits: one 7B model and one 15,119-example adaptation set; the tasks are commonsense reasoning, with no instruction following, multilingual or long-context coverage; ablations only vary TT rank and adapter placement; no production deployment is reported.
What it does not claim: state of the art for every PEFT setting; a wholesale replacement for DoRA; a full training-throughput or memory evaluation; a result that extrapolates across model scales.
