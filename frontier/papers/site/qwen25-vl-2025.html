多模态Multimodal

Qwen2.5-VL Technical ReportQwen2.5-VL Technical Report

问题：视觉语言模型常把图片缩放到固定分辨率、视频按固定帧率采样，导致小字、表格、长视频事件定位失真；能不能原生长分辨率、原生长时序。
证据位置：摘要提出动态分辨率处理与绝对时间编码，支持不同尺寸图像与长达数小时视频，并做到秒级事件定位；从零训练原生动态分辨率 ViT 并加 Window Attention；提供三个尺寸，旗舰 72B 在文档/图表理解上对标 GPT-4o 与 Claude 3.5 Sonnet。
作者自陈局限：对标结论集中在文档与图表；72B 是旗舰，未说明小尺寸差距；视频评测与真实部署仍有距离；没有独立复现与完整安全评估。
它没主张的：不是所有视觉任务都超过 GPT-4o；不是小时级视频都能精准定位；不是三个尺寸性能相同。Problem: vision-language models often resize images to a fixed resolution and sample video at a fixed rate, hurting small text, tables and event localisation; can resolution and time be native.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract introduces dynamic-resolution processing and absolute time encoding, supporting varied image sizes and videos up to hours with second-level event localisation; a native dynamic-resolution ViT is trained from scratch with Window Attention; three sizes are offered, and the 72B flagship matches GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on document and diagram understanding.
What the authors say about limits: the comparison focuses on documents and diagrams; 72B is the flagship and the gap at smaller sizes is not detailed; video evaluation is not the same as production deployment; no independent reproduction or full safety evaluation.
What it does not claim: not a win over GPT-4o on every vision task; not second-level localisation in every hour-long video; not identical performance across the three sizes.
