多模态Multimodal

UniM: A Unified Any-to-Any Interleaved Multimodal BenchmarkUniM: A Unified Any-to-Any Interleaved Multimodal Benchmark

问题：真实多模态应用要读任意组合、交错排列的输入，还要输出交错的多媒体内容；已有基准多停在单轮、双模态，测不到这种能力。
证据位置：摘要给出 UniM：31K 条高质量实例、30 个领域、7 种模态（文本、图像、音频、视频、文档、代码、3D）；评测分三维：语义正确性与生成质量、响应结构完整性、交错连贯性；另给出带可追踪推理的 UniMA 基线。
作者自陈局限：31K 是构建的数据集，不是持续更新的真实流量；7 模态仍缺触觉、动作等；三维评分如何自动判定、人工一致性如何，摘要未展开；UniMA 只是基线，不是能力上限。
它没主张的：不是新模型；不是排行榜；不是证明现有模型已解决任意到任意；不是评测全部多模态任务。Problem: real multimodal applications must read arbitrarily combined, interleaved inputs and produce interleaved multimedia outputs; existing benchmarks mostly stop at single-turn, bimodal settings and cannot measure this.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract presents UniM with 31K high-quality instances across 30 domains and seven modalities (text, image, audio, video, document, code and 3D); it scores models on semantic correctness and generation quality, response-structure integrity, and interleaved coherence; it also provides UniMA, an agentic baseline with traceable reasoning.
What the authors say about limits: 31K is a constructed dataset, not continuously refreshed real traffic; seven modalities still omit touch, action and others; how the three dimensions are scored automatically and how consistent human judgements are is not detailed in the abstract; UniMA is a baseline, not a capability ceiling.
What it does not claim: a new model; a leaderboard; proof that current models have solved any-to-any; coverage of every multimodal task.
