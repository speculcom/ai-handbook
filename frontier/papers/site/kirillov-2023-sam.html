架构Architecture

SAM：可提示的分割一切模型Segment Anything

问题：分割任务通常按语义/实例/全景分开训练，换任务就要换模型；能不能定义一个「给提示就出掩码」的通用任务，用一个模型加提示工程覆盖多种分割？
证据位置：任务、模型与数据分别在 §3–§5；数据引擎与 SA-1B 在 §4；零样本评测在 §6–§7（23 个分割数据集、边缘检测、候选框、实例分割、文本到掩码）；局限集中在 §8，结论紧随其后。
作者自陈局限：§8 列了六条——会漏细结构，偶尔幻觉出小的不连通部件，边界不如「放大再算」的方法锐利；给很多点时，专用交互式分割方法会更好；重编码器使整体并非实时（虽然提示解码是实时的）；文本到掩码只是探索性尝试，不够稳健；如何用简单提示实现语义与全景分割仍不清楚；在特定领域，专用工具会优于 SAM。
它没主张的：没有说 SAM 在所有分割任务上超过专用模型——§8 明确说专用交互式方法与领域工具会更好；没有说文本到掩码可用——原文称 exploratory and not entirely robust；也没有说「分割一切」字面成立——论文自己把任务限定为可提示分割，并承认语义/全景分割的提示设计未解决。Problem: segmentation is usually split into semantic, instance and panoptic tasks with separate models; can a single “give a prompt, get a mask” task plus prompt engineering cover many segmentation settings?
Where the evidence sits: task, model and data in Sections 3-5; data engine and SA-1B in Section 4; zero-shot evaluation in Sections 6-7 (23 segmentation datasets, edge detection, object proposals, instance segmentation, text-to-mask); limitations in Section 8, immediately before the conclusion.
Stated limits: Section 8 lists six — SAM misses fine structures, occasionally hallucinates small disconnected components, and produces less crisp boundaries than methods that zoom in; dedicated interactive segmentation methods outperform it when many points are given; the heavy image encoder means overall performance is not real-time even though prompt decoding is; text-to-mask is exploratory and not robust; simple prompts for semantic and panoptic segmentation remain unclear; and domain-specific tools will beat SAM in their domains.
What it does not claim: it does not claim SAM beats specialised models on every segmentation task — Section 8 says dedicated interactive methods and domain tools do better; it does not claim text-to-mask is usable, calling it exploratory and not entirely robust; and it does not claim “segment anything” literally, framing the task as promptable segmentation and leaving semantic/panoptic prompting unresolved.
