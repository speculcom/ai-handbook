FlashAttention-3: Fast and Accurate Attention with Asynchrony and Low-precisionFlashAttention-3: Fast and Accurate Attention with Asynchrony and Low-precision

问题：注意力是长上下文模型的核心瓶颈；FlashAttention 系列已减少显存读写，但在 Hopper 上仍未吃满 Tensor Core、TMA 与 FP8 的硬件能力。
证据位置：摘要指出 FlashAttention-2 在 H100 上只有 35% 利用率；FlashAttention-3 用 warp-specialization、块级 matmul/softmax 交叠与 FP8 块量化，把 FP16 推到最高 740 TFLOPs/s（75% 利用率），FP8 接近 1.2 PFLOPs/s，速度提升 1.5–2.0 倍，且 FP8 数值误差比基线低 2.6 倍。
作者自陈局限：优化针对 Hopper（H100）与特定 kernel；不是所有 GPU 架构都能获得同样收益；测的是注意力算子，不是端到端模型吞吐；FP8 精度收益依赖具体模型与任务。
它没主张的：不是所有注意力实现都需要重写；不是 FP8 在所有模型上都无损；不是 75% 利用率等于端到端推理快 2 倍。Problem: attention is the core bottleneck for long-context models; FlashAttention reduced memory traffic but still left Hopper Tensor Cores, TMA and FP8 underused.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract notes FlashAttention-2 reaches only 35% utilization on H100; FlashAttention-3 uses warp specialization, interleaved block-wise matmul/softmax and FP8 block quantization to reach up to 740 TFLOPs/s in FP16 (75% utilization) and close to 1.2 PFLOPs/s in FP8, with a 1.5–2.0× speedup and 2.6× lower FP8 numerical error than a baseline.
What the authors say about limits: the optimisation targets Hopper (H100) and specific kernels; other GPU architectures do not automatically get the same gain; it measures the attention operator, not end-to-end model throughput; FP8 accuracy depends on model and task.
What it does not claim: not that every attention implementation must be rewritten; not that FP8 is lossless for every model; not that 75% utilization means 2× end-to-end inference.
