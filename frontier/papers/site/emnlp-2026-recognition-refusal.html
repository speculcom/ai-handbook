推理Reasoning

Recognition–Refusal Misalignment in LLMsRecognition–Refusal Misalignment in LLMs

问题：模型明明「知道」一个问题不可答，为什么还是会编一个答案？是表征没编码「不可答」，还是编码了却没接到拒答行为上？
证据位置：论文在隐藏状态里找到一条线性方向，能把可答与「结构上不可能」的数学/代码题分开；识别方向与安全拒答方向接近正交，因此作者把问题解释为路由失败，而不是编码失败。
作者自陈局限：实验集中在数学与代码的结构性不可答题，不是所有「不知道」；线性探针能分开，不等于模型在生成时一定使用这条方向；拒答还受提示、语言与安全策略影响；论文没有给出通用修复方案。
它没主张的：不是所有幻觉都来自路由；不是「加一个拒答头」就能解决；不是对安全对齐的全面评估；不是跨语言、跨领域的普适结论。Problem: if a model represents that a question is unanswerable, why does it still invent an answer? Is the representation missing, or is it encoded but not routed to refusal?
Where the evidence sits: the paper finds a single linear direction in hidden states that separates answerable from structurally impossible math and code prompts; the recognition direction is close to orthogonal to the safety-refusal direction, so the authors explain the failure as routing rather than encoding.
What the authors say about limits: experiments focus on structurally unanswerable math and code, not every kind of “don’t know”; a linear probe separating the directions does not prove the model uses that direction when generating; refusal also depends on prompts, language and safety policy; the paper offers no general fix.
What it does not claim: that every hallucination is a routing failure; that adding a refusal head solves it; a comprehensive evaluation of safety alignment; a universal cross-language, cross-domain conclusion.
