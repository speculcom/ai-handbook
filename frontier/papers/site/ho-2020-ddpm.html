DDPM：去噪扩散概率模型Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

问题：GAN 能出高质量样本但训练不稳定，VAE 有似然但样本偏模糊；能不能用「逐步加噪、再逐步去噪」的扩散过程，同时拿到质量和可训练性？
证据位置：前向/反向过程在 §2–§3，训练目标（简化噪声预测损失）在 §3，采样在 Algorithm 2；实验在 §4，CIFAR10 的 IS/FID 在 Table 1，LSUN 256×256 在 §4.4 左右；结论在 §6，影响讨论在 Broader Impact。
作者自陈局限：论文没有独立 Limitations 章节。摘要与 §1 明确说「样本质量好，但似然不如其他似然模型有竞争力」，并解释大部分无损码长花在不可感知的细节上（§4.3）；Broader Impact 主动写了两类风险——可被用于伪造公众人物图像与视频，以及训练数据偏见会被生成模型放大。
它没主张的：没有说扩散模型在似然上优于 ARM/VAE；没有说采样已经够快——扩散需要多步迭代，这一点是后续工作（DDIM、蒸馏等）才改善；也没有说 FID 3.17 可以跨数据集比较——FID 依赖特征提取器与样本量，论文只报了自己的设置。Problem: GANs produce sharp samples but train unstably, while VAEs have likelihoods but blurrier samples; can a process that gradually adds noise and then denoises it deliver both quality and trainability?
Where the evidence sits: forward and reverse processes in Sections 2-3, the simplified noise-prediction objective in Section 3, sampling in Algorithm 2; experiments in Section 4, with CIFAR10 IS/FID in Table 1 and 256x256 LSUN around 4.4; conclusion in Section 6 and societal impact in the Broader Impact section.
Stated limits: there is no separate Limitations section. The abstract and Section 1 state plainly that sample quality is high but likelihoods are not competitive with other likelihood-based models, explaining that most lossless code length goes to imperceptible detail (Section 4.3); the Broader Impact section volunteers two risks — use for fake images and videos of public figures, and amplification of dataset biases.
What it does not claim: it does not claim diffusion beats autoregressive models or VAEs on likelihood; it does not claim sampling is fast — diffusion needs many iterative steps, which later work (DDIM, distillation) addressed; and it does not claim FID 3.17 is comparable across datasets, since FID depends on the feature extractor and sample size.
