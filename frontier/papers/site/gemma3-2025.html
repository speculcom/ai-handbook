多模态Multimodal

Gemma 3 Technical ReportGemma 3 Technical Report

问题：轻量开源模型要同时具备视觉、多语言与长上下文，但长上下文会让 KV cache 膨胀；能不能靠局部/全局注意力配比，在 1B–27B 上把能力与显存同时守住。
证据位置：摘要给出 1B–27B 的轻量多模态系列，增加视觉理解、语言覆盖与至少 128K 上下文；通过提高局部注意力层比例、缩短局部分窗来压 KV cache；用蒸馏训练，Gemma3-4B-IT 对标 Gemma2-27B-IT，Gemma3-27B-IT 对标 Gemini-1.5-Pro，全部模型开放。
作者自陈局限：最大只到 27B；对标来自自建评测；视觉能力与长上下文的组合任务覆盖有限；没有完整安全评估与生产部署数据。
它没主张的：不是 27B 全面超过更大闭源模型；不是 128K 对所有任务都有效；不是开源权重就等于无许可限制。Problem: lightweight open models need vision, multilingual coverage and long context, but long context inflates the KV cache; can the local/global attention ratio keep capability and memory within reach at 1B–27B.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract covers a 1B–27B lightweight multimodal family with vision understanding, wider language coverage and at least 128K context; it raises the ratio of local to global attention layers and shortens the local span to reduce KV-cache memory; distillation training makes Gemma3-4B-IT competitive with Gemma2-27B-IT and Gemma3-27B-IT comparable to Gemini-1.5-Pro, with all models released.
What the authors say about limits: the largest model is 27B; comparisons come from their own evaluation; combined vision and long-context tasks have limited coverage; there is no full safety evaluation or production deployment data.
What it does not claim: not that 27B beats larger closed models everywhere; not that 128K works for every task; not that open weights mean no licence constraints.
