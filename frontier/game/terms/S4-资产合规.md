# S4 · Asset licensing

**这一步决定什么**：能不能合法发布。这是唯一一步做错了会让整个项目作废的。

<!-- EN -->
> What this stage decides: whether you can legally ship. This is the only step whose failure voids the entire project.

## 为什么单独拿出来讲

前面的步骤做错了，改代码就行。

⚠ 更麻烦的是：AI 生成的资产**商用授权通常不明确**。
模型训练用了什么数据、输出能不能商用，取决于服务条款，条款随时会变。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Why it gets its own stage
>
> Earlier stages can be fixed by editing code. Getting this one wrong can get you taken down.
>
> ⚠ Worse: AI-generated assets usually have unclear commercial rights. What data a model was trained on, and whether you may sell its output, comes down to the service terms — and terms change.

## 三类风险

| 资产类型 | 风险等级 | 关键问题 |
|---|---|---|
| **AI 生成的图像/音频** | ⚠ **中-高** | 服务条款是否授予商用权？条款改过吗？ |
|  | 低 | 确认是 CC0（不是 CC-BY，后者要署名） |
| **付费素材包** | 低 | 买的是「商用」还是「个人」？看授权范围 |
| **从别处「借」来的** | ⚠ **高** | 没授权就是侵权，AI 改一下不算 |

<!-- EN -->
> ## Three kinds of risk
>
> | Asset type | Risk | The question that matters |
> |---|---|---|
> | AI-generated image or audio | ⚠  | Do the terms grant commercial rights? Have the terms changed since? |
> | CC0 / public domain | Low | Confirm it is really CC0 (not CC-BY, which requires attribution) |
> |  | Low | Did you buy commercial or personal? Read the licence scope |
> | "Borrowed" from elsewhere | ⚠ **High** | No licence is infringement, and running it through AI does not change that |

## 实操做法

**1. 建一个资产台账**

哪怕是一个表格，记三列：

| 文件 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.png` | 自己画的 | 本人原创 |
| `bgm.mp3` | 生成式音乐工具 X | ⚠ 待确认商用条款 |
| `coin.wav` | freesound.org | CC0 |

⚠ 台账里出现「待确认」的条目，在发布前必须清零。

**2. 优先选低风险来源**

- 图像：**程序化生成**（代码画的图形）风险最低：版权归你；用生成图就注意整包风格一致，见 [美术风格的一致性](./美术风格的一致性.html)
- 音频：**自己做的音效**比生成的音乐安全；要现成的就认 CC0 / CC-BY / NC 三种标记，见 [音频素材的来源与授权](./音频素材的来源与授权.html)
- 字体：**注意授权**：很多字体不能商用



⚠ **CC-BY 需要署名**。用了 CC-BY 素材而没署名，可能违反协议。

<!-- EN -->
> ## How to do it in practice
>
> 1. Keep an asset ledger
>
> Even a plain table with three columns:
>
> | File | Source | Licence |
> |---|---|---|
> | `hero.png` | drawn by me | my own work |
> | `bgm.mp3` | generative music tool X | ⚠ commercial terms unconfirmed |
> | `coin.wav` | freesound.org | CC0 |
>
> ⚠ Every "unconfirmed" row must be cleared before you ship.
>
> 2. Prefer low-risk sources
>
> - Images: procedural generation (code-drawn shapes) is safest — the copyright is yours; if you generate art, mind pack-wide style consistency — see [Keeping the art style consistent](./美术风格的一致性.html)
> - Audio: sound effects you record yourself are safer than generated music; for ready-made packs learn the CC0 / CC-BY / NC marks — see [Audio sources and their licences](./音频素材的来源与授权.html)
> - Fonts:  — many fonts are not commercial-use
>
> 3. Mind attribution when mixing
>
> ⚠ CC-BY requires attribution. Using CC-BY material without crediting it may breach the licence.

## AI 生成资产：许可与来源多数不完整

本站的判断（**没有实测，只有推理**）：

- **单张/单组**素材可用
- **成套资产的风格一致性**是当前生成模型的弱项，不是提示词问题
- **商用授权**在多数服务里**存在但条款模糊**，且可能随时变更

所以本站的建议是：AI 资产用来做原型，正式发布前替换或逐条确认授权。

<!-- EN -->
> ## AI-generated assets: licences and provenance are often incomplete
>
> This site's judgement (no measurement, only reasoning):
>
> - Individual assets or small sets are usable
> - Style consistency across a full set is a current weakness of image models — and that is not a prompting problem
> - , and may change
>
> So this site's advice: use AI assets for the prototype, and replace them or verify each licence before shipping.

## 常见失败

- ：模型的训练数据来源往往不明确
- **抄了素材包的图**：付费素材包的授权是「一个人一个项目」，不是「随便用」
- **忘了字体授权**：⚠ 这是最常被忽略的一项
- **没留台账**：到要发布时才发现说不清哪张图哪来的

<!-- EN -->
> ## Common failures
>
> - Assuming "AI made it, so it's mine" — the training data's provenance is usually unclear
> - Taking images from an asset pack — a paid pack usually licenses one person, one project, not "use anywhere"
> - Forgetting font licences — ⚠ the most frequently missed one
> -  — at release time you realise you cannot say where any given image came from

## 判断你这一步做完了吗

- [ ] 每份资产都能说出来源
- [ ] 台账里没有「待确认」
- [ ] ⚠ 字体授权已确认

<!-- EN -->

>
> - [ ] Every asset can name its source
> - [ ] The ledger has no "unconfirmed" rows left
> - [ ] ⚠ Font licences are confirmed
