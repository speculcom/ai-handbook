# Font licensing traps

**一句话字体是唯一被系统性忽略的一项。**

<!-- EN -->
> 

## 字体许可按嵌入方式分档

| 特征 | 图片/音频 | **字体** |
|---|---|---|
| 一眼能否看出是 AI 生成/抓来的 | ✅ 通常能 | ⚠ 看不出来，就是个字体文件 |
| 授权是否明确写在素材里 | ✅ | ⚠ 常藏在官网的「条款」页深处 |
| ⚠ 侵权后果 | 可能被要求下架 | ⚠  |

<!-- EN -->
> ## Font licences depend on embedding method
>
> | Property | Images / audio | **Fonts** |
> |---|---|---|
> | Can you tell at a glance that it was AI-generated or taken? | Usually | ⚠ No — it is just a font file |
> | Is the licence stated inside the asset? | Yes | ⚠ Often buried deep on the provider's terms page |
> | ⚠ Consequence of infringement | Possibly a takedown | ⚠ Possibly an order to stop distributing every product |

## 免费档通常不含商用

字体站最常见的授权写法是：

```
✅ 个人使用免费
✅ 商业使用需购买授权
⚠ 但常常没说清楚：
   - 「个人」包不包括个人网站？
   - App 内嵌算商业吗？
   - ⚠ 游戏内嵌呢？
```

⚠ 同一个字体在不同使用场景下，授权可能完全不同。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Free tiers usually exclude commercial use
>
> The most common wording on a font site is:
>
> ```
> ✅ Free for personal use
> ✅ Commercial use requires a paid licence
> ⚠ But it rarely clarifies:
>    - Does "personal" include a personal website?
>    - Does embedding it in an app count as commercial?
>    - ⚠ What about embedding it in a game?
> ```
>
> ⚠ The same font can be licensed completely differently depending on the use case.

## 三个安全的做法



⚠ 注意区分「开源字体」与「免费字体」：前者授权清晰（OFL/Apache 等），
后者只是「不收你钱」。

**2. 程序化生成字体**

⚠ 用代码画字形（比如用矢量路径生成像素字体）：版权归你，没有授权问题。
⚠ 缺点是不好看，但至少不会出事。

**3. 用引擎内置字体**

⚠ Godot / Unity 等自带默认字体，**授权随引擎**（通常是引擎许可覆盖）。
⚠ 但要注意：引擎许可是否覆盖字体，要看引擎的官方说明：别默认。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Three safe approaches
>
> 1. Pick fonts whose licence is explicitly open source
>
> ⚠ Distinguish an  from a **free font** — the former has a clear licence (OFL, Apache, …), the latter merely charges you nothing.
>
> 2. Generate the glyphs programmatically
>
> ⚠ Drawing letterforms with code (for example generating a pixel font from vector paths) makes the copyright yours. ⚠ It looks worse, but it cannot get you into trouble.
>
> 3. Use the engine's built-in font
>
> ⚠ Godot, Unity and others ship a default font whose licence follows the engine's. ⚠ Check the engine's official statement that the licence covers the font — do not assume it.

## 实操

在资产台账里给字体单列一行：

| 文件 | 来源 | 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| `ui.ttf` | 某某字体站 | ⚠ 个人免费：**未确认商用** |

⚠ 「未确认商用」在发布前必须清零。见 [AI 资产台账](./ai-资产台账.html)。

<!-- EN -->
> ## In practice
>
> Give fonts their own row in the asset ledger:
>
> | File | Source | Licence |
> |---|---|---|
> | `ui.ttf` | some font site | ⚠ free for personal use — commercial use unconfirmed |
>
> ⚠ "Commercial use unconfirmed" must be cleared before shipping. See the [AI asset ledger](./ai-资产台账.html).
