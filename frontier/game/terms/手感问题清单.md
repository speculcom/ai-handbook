# Game feel checklist

**一句话改错参数等于白改。**

<!-- EN -->
> 

## 先诊断

⚠ **顺序很重要**：先确认它是手感问题还是代码问题。

| 症状 | 先查这个 | 再调这个 |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠ **按键后有延迟感** | **输入缓冲有没有？** | ⚠ 很可能不是手感问题 |
| **飘** | 重力值 | 增大重力；落地加一点下压 |
| **粘** | 空中控制是否过强 | 减小跳跃初速；加 coyote time |
|  |
| ⚠ **边缘判定模糊** | **碰撞盒是不是比视觉大** | 玩家碰撞盒通常该小一点 |
| **操作延迟响应** | 动画是否阻塞了输入 | 动画期间不应锁输入 |

<!-- EN -->
> ## Diagnose first
>
> ⚠ : establish whether it is a feel problem or a code problem.
>
> | Symptom | Check this first | Then tune |
> |---|---|---|
> | ⚠ Lag after a keypress | Is there an input buffer? | ⚠ It is probably not a feel problem at all |
> | **Floaty** | The gravity value | Raise gravity; add a little downward force on landing |
> | **Sticky** | Is air control too strong | Lower jump velocity; add coyote time |
> |  |
> | ⚠  | Is the collider larger than the sprite? | A player's hitbox is usually smaller |
> |  | Is animation blocking input | Input should not be locked during animation |

## 三个最常被忽略的参数

| 参数 | 作用 | 忽略它的后果 |
|---|---|---|
| **coyote time** | 离开平台后仍可跳的宽限（帧） | 玩家会感觉「我明明按了」 |
| **jump buffer** | 落地前按跳会缓存（帧） | 同上 |
| **可变跳跃高度** | 松开跳跃键时截断上升 | 每次跳跃都一样高 → 失去控制感 |

⚠ 这三个是「操作手感」的地基。它们不解决就会一直觉得不对劲，但你可能说不出为什么。

<!-- EN -->
> ## The three most forgotten parameters
>
> | Parameter | What it does | What happens without it |
> |---|---|---|
> | **coyote time** | Grace window after leaving a platform during which you can still jump (in frames) | Players feel "but I definitely pressed it" |
> | **jump buffer** | A jump pressed before landing is remembered (in frames) | Same |
> | Variable jump height | Releasing the button cuts the rise short | Every jump is the same height → loss of control |
>
> ⚠ These three are the foundation of control feel. Without them something always feels off, and you may not be able to say why.

## 手感问题不能靠加动画解决

> 大部分「手感不对」是数值问题，不是代码问题。

把代码重写三遍不如把重力从 980 调到 1150 试一次。

⚠ 而且改完要**每次只改一个**：一次改三个，你就不知道是哪个起的作用，
下次遇到相似问题还是不知道怎么调。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Game feel cannot be fixed by adding animation
>
> > Most "it does not feel right" cases are a numbers problem, not a code problem.
>
> Rewriting the code three times is worse than trying gravity 1150 instead of 980 once.
>
> ⚠ And change one thing at a time — change three and you will not know which one mattered, so the next time you meet a similar problem you still cannot tune it.

## 判断你可以进入下一阶段了吗

- [ ] 别人玩过，**没有问「这游戏怎么玩」**
- [ ] 你自己玩 30 分钟不觉得无聊
- [ ] 关键参数是**可调**的（不是硬编码）

<!-- EN -->
> ## Can you move to the next stage?
>
> - [ ] Someone else played it and did not ask "how do I play this?"
> - [ ] You have played it for 30 minutes and still find it interesting
> - [ ] The key parameters are **adjustable** rather than hard-coded
