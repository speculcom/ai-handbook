# Audio sources and their licences

**一句话授权类型**：CC0、CC-BY、NC（禁商用）长得都很像，后果完全不同。

<!-- EN -->
>  — CC0, CC-BY and NC (no commercial use) look alike and mean very different things.

## 三种授权，先学会认

| 标记 | 能商用吗 | 要署名吗 | 典型来源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CC0**（公有领域） | ✅ | 不需要 | Kenney 全部素材、Freesound 部分条目 |
| **CC-BY 4.0** | ✅ | ⚠ **必须署名** | OpenGameArt 大量条目 |
| ⚠ **含 NC 条款**（NonCommercial） | ❌ 不能商用 | 不适用 | Freesound 大量条目 |

⚠ 最容易犯的错：在素材站看到「免费」就用。
Freesound 的官方 FAQ 明确写着：条目各有不同许可，含 NonCommercial 条款的不能商用（出处见下表）。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Three licences — learn to tell them apart
>
> | Mark | Commercial OK | Attribution | Typical source |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **CC0** (public domain) | Yes | Not required | All of Kenney's assets, some Freesound entries |
> | **CC-BY 4.0** | Yes | ⚠ Attribution required | Many OpenGameArt entries |
> | ⚠ **NC clause** (NonCommercial) | ❌ Not commercial | — | Many Freesound entries |
>
> ⚠ The classic mistake: seeing "free" on an asset site and using it, missing the small NC mark next to it.
> Freesound's own FAQ states plainly that entries carry different licences and those with a NonCommercial clause cannot be used commercially (source in the table below).

## 署名不是可选项

用了 CC-BY 素材，署名要**真的进游戏**：

- 网页游戏：credits 页或 itch.io 页面描述里写明素材名、作者、许可链接
- 商店发布：设置页或片尾滚动里放同样内容

⚠ 只写在开发文档里不算：玩家看不到就等于没署（这是 CC-BY 的常见违约方式）。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Attribution is not optional
>
> If you use CC-BY material, the credit has to actually ship in the game:
>
> - Web games: name the asset, author and licence link on a credits page or the itch.io page description
> - Store releases: the same content in a settings screen or end credits
>
> ⚠ Writing it only in dev docs does not count — if players cannot see it, it is a breach (the most common CC-BY violation).

<!-- EN -->
> ## Generated music: read the terms before the ledger
>
