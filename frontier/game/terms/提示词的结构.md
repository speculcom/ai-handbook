# Structuring the prompt

**一句话**：AI 写不出能跑的游戏，多半不是因为它不会，而是因为你没说清要什么。

<!-- EN -->
> **In one line**: AI usually fails to produce a runnable game not because it cannot code, but because you did not say what you wanted.

## 三样必须给的

| 要给 | 为什么 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **输入 → 行为映射** | 不给会得到「按 WASD 移动」这种废话 | 「左键跳跃，Shift 冲刺，R 重开」 |
| **具体数值** | 不给会得到一堆 `TODO` | 「跳跃初速 -420，重力 980」 |
| ⚠ **明确的「不要做什么」** |  | 「这一版不要敌人、不要 UI、不要动画」 |

⚠ 第三条最容易漏，而且**影响最大**。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Three things you must give
>
> | Give | Why | Example |
> |---|---|---|
> | An input → behaviour mapping | Without it you get filler like "move with WASD" | "Left click jumps, Shift dashes, R restarts" |
> |  | Without them you get a pile of `TODO`s | "Jump velocity -420, gravity 980" |
> | ⚠ An explicit "do not do X" | Without it you get one giant file containing every system imaginable | "This version: no enemies, no UI, no animation" |
>
> ⚠ The third is the easiest to forget, and it has the .

## 需求写成一段话，AI 会漏约束

你说：「做一个平台跳跃游戏」

AI 会给你：玩家、敌人、关卡、UI、动画、存档、音效、暂停菜单：**全部都有，全部没做深**。

然后你在调试器里花三天找「为什么角色会掉出屏幕」。

你得到的不是少功能，而是一堆半成品。

<!-- EN -->
> ## A one-paragraph brief makes AI miss constraints
>
> You say: "make a platformer".
>
> AI gives you: player, enemies, levels, UI, animation, saves, sound effects, pause menu — all present, none finished.
>
> Then you spend three days in a debugger looking for why the character falls off the screen.
>
> What you get is not fewer features — it is a pile of half-finished ones.

## 更好的写法

```
写 Godot GDScript，一个最小可玩的原型：
- 玩家：CharacterBody2D，左右移动 + 跳跃
- 数值：重力 980，跳跃初速 -420，coyote time 100ms
- 地面用 StaticBody2D，碰撞层设 layer 1 / mask 1
- ⚠ 不要：敌人、UI、动画、存档、音效，次只做移动与跳跃
```

⚠ 注意最后一行。

<!-- EN -->
> ## A better prompt
>
> ```
> Write Godot GDScript for a minimal playable prototype:
> - Player: CharacterBody2D with left/right movement and jumping
> - Numbers: gravity 980, jump velocity -420, coyote time 100 ms
> - Ground uses StaticBody2D, collision layer 1 / mask 1
> - ⚠ Do not: enemies, UI, animation, saves, sound effects — this round is movement and jumping only
> ```
>
> ⚠ Note the last line. It saves far more time than the words it costs.

## 迭代的节奏

⚠ **不要一次要全部**。正确的顺序：

1. 第一次：只做移动 → 跑起来
2. 第二次：加跳跃 → 跑起来
3. 第三次：加相机跟随 → 跑起来
4. 第四次：才加视觉

⚠ 每一轮都应该是**「能跑的东西」**，不是「一部分代码」。

<!-- EN -->
> ## The rhythm of iteration
>
> ⚠ Do not ask for everything at once. The right order:
>
> 1. First round: movement only → it runs
> 2. Second: add jumping → it runs
> 3. Third: add camera follow → it runs
> 4. Fourth: only then add visuals
>
> ⚠ Each round should produce "something that runs", not "part of the code".
