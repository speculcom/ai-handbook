# S5 · Ship it

**这一步决定什么**：让人真的玩到。AI 在这一步几乎帮不上忙，很正常。

<!-- EN -->
> What this stage decides: getting it in front of players. AI is almost no help here — and that is normal.

## 为什么 AI 在这里作用最小

导出是**确定性工程**：固定的流程、固定的工具链、明确的成功条件。

| 环节 | AI 能做什么 |
|---|---|
| 配置导出参数 | ✅ 能给模板 |
| 解决导出报错 | ⚠ 能猜，但报错信息往往不完整 |
| 平台审核合规 | ❌ 需要自己查 |
| 商店页面文案 | ✅ 能写 |
| **实际测试每种设备** | ❌ 做不了 |

⚠ 这不是 AI 的缺陷。导出是工程问题，工程问题的解法是查文档，不是让模型猜。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Why AI helps least here
>
> Exporting is deterministic engineering: a fixed process, a fixed toolchain, an unambiguous success condition.
>
> | Stage | What AI can do |
> |---|---|
> | Configure export settings | ✅ It can give a template |
> | Fix an export error | ⚠ It can guess, but error messages are often incomplete |
> | Platform review compliance | ❌ You must check this yourself |
> | Store page copy | ✅ It can write it |
> | Actually testing on each device | ❌ It cannot |
>
> ⚠ This is not a flaw of AI. Engineering problems are solved by reading the docs, not by asking a model to guess.

| 平台 | 门槛 | ⚠ 常见的坑 |
|---|---|---|
|  | 最低 | 浏览器差异（Safari 的音频、WebGL 版本） |
|  | 中 | 签名与公证（macOS Gatekeeper） |

>

>
> | Platform | Barrier | ⚠ Common traps |

## 发布前必须自己测的

AI 帮不了、也必须做的：

- [ ] **在目标设备上真的跑过**：不是在开发机上
- [ ] **首次加载时间**：网页游戏尤其重要
- [ ] **音量/静音状态**：⚠ 忘记检查这个的开发者很多
- [ ] **存档是否真的保存**：清理浏览器数据后重试
- [ ] ⚠ **离线/断网行为**：是崩溃还是优雅降级？

<!-- EN -->
> ## What AI cannot do and you must
>
> - [ ] You ran it on the target device — not just the dev machine
> - [ ]  — especially for web games
> - [ ] Volume / mute state — ⚠ many developers forget this one
> - [ ] Whether saves actually persist — retry after clearing browser data
> - [ ] ⚠ Offline / no-network behaviour — does it crash or degrade gracefully?

## 先发一个能玩的极简版

> **先发一个「极简版」。**

在 itch.io 上传一个能玩的、但素材很粗糙的版本，然后看有没有人玩。

⚠ 理由，个行业的最大风险不是「游戏不好玩」，而是**你不知道它好不好玩**。
与其做完 80% 再发现方向错了，不如花 20% 做一个能验证方向的版本。

：因为原型成本降到了可以「多发几个试」的程度。

<!-- EN -->
> ## Ship a playable minimal version first
>
> > Ship an extremely rough version first.
>
> Upload something playable on itch.io, with deliberately crude assets, and see whether anyone plays it.
>
> ⚠ The reason: the biggest risk in this industry is not "the game is bad" — it is that you do not know whether the game is bad. Rather than discovering the wrong direction after 80% is done, spend 20% on something that validates it.
>
> AI makes this strategy affordable — because prototype cost has dropped enough to "ship a few and try".

## 常见失败

- **追求「做完再发」**：⚠ 最常见的失败模式
- **发给朋友**：⚠ 朋友会给你礼貌性反馈，陌生人才给你真实反馈
- **只在开发机上测**：开发机的显卡和真实用户差得远
- ：网页游戏的最大流失点
- **第一次发布就上商店**：审核周期会让你失去调整的窗口

<!-- EN -->
> ## Common failures
>
> - Aiming to finish before shipping — ⚠ the most common failure mode
> - Sending it to friends — ⚠ friends give you polite feedback; strangers give you real feedback
> - Testing only on the dev machine — its GPU is nothing like a real user's
> - Ignoring Safari / older browsers — the biggest leak for web games
> - Going straight to a store on the first release — the review cycle removes your window to adjust

## 判断你这一步做完了吗

- [ ] 在**非开发机**上跑过
- [ ] 有人（不是你）玩过并给你反馈
- [ ] ⚠ 存档、离线、音量都测过

<!-- EN -->
> ## Are you done with this stage?
>
> - [ ] It runs on a machine that is not your dev machine
> - [ ] Someone who is not you has played it and given you feedback
> - [ ] ⚠ Saves, offline behaviour and volume have all been tested
