这件作品About this piece

演示什么：Carbon 在 Antigravity 里按一篇学术论文做了交互式多尺度织物可视化：虚拟纤维有限元框架、可交互的 3D 纱线网格，并把材料结构分层展示。官方怎么说：Google 把它列为长程任务示例，强调从论文到可操作模型。边界：官方摘录未给完整参数与论文核对；这是可视化，不是材料性能验证。What it shows: in Antigravity, Carbon built an interactive multiscale textile visualiser from an academic paper — a virtual-fibre finite-element framework with interactive 3D yarn meshes, showing material structure across scales. What the page says: Google lists it as a long-horizon example, stressing paper-to-interactive-model execution. Boundary: the excerpt gives no full parameters or paper check; it is a visualisation, not a materials-performance validation.
