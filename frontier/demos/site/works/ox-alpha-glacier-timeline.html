这件作品About this piece

一条用同一座程序化山谷讲完的冰川退却时间轴：拖动 1900–2026 年份滑杆，冰舌持续变短变薄，冰碛垄从退却末端露出，绿松石色冰前湖扩张，草甸沿谷底上爬；场景内钉着「首次测量照片」「A-4 花杆」等里程碑，数据卡实时显示冰面积 14.2→4.1 km²、退却米数与湖泊公顷，PLAY CENTURY 自动播放 126 年，WINTER 叠加季节雪。看点是关键帧状态表逐帧插值。边界：示意性重建，不是实测冰川记录；数字为情景值。A glacier-retreat timeline told through one procedural valley: drag the 1900–2026 year slider and the ice tongue shortens and thins, moraine ridges emerge behind the terminus, a turquoise proglacial lake swells and meadow creeps up the floor. Milestone flags mark “First survey photo” and “Stake A-4”; a data card tracks ice area from 14.2 to 4.1 km², retreat metres and lake hectares. PLAY CENTURY runs 126 years, WINTER adds seasonal snow. The craft is a keyframe state table interpolated per frame. Boundary: an illustrative reconstruction, not measured glacier data; figures are scenario values.
