Gemini 4 教人形机器人投沙包Teaching a humanoid robot to play cornhole with Gemini 4 Argon

这件作品About this piece

演示什么：Google DeepMind 的机器人团队让 Gemini 4 Argon 看一段人投沙包的视频，模型在 MuJoCo 里搭出虚拟仿真，算出物理与全身控制参数，再交给 Apptronik 的 Apollo 2 人形机器人执行；官方称它在真实世界连续投进 7 球。 官方怎么说：这条 Video-to-Sim-to-Real 流水线把「几周的手工建模」压到「几小时」，目标是让普通视频变成物理 AI 的训练场。 边界：这是内部测试的单个任务，不是跨任务成功率；视频没有给出尝试次数、失败分布、 sim-to-real 偏差或安全边界，不能外推成「人形机器人已通用」。What it shows: Google DeepMind robotics researchers had Gemini 4 Argon watch a video of a person playing cornhole; the model built a MuJoCo simulation, calculated the physics and whole-body control, and handed them to Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoid, which the official video says sank seven consecutive shots in the real world. What the official video says: this Video-to-Sim-to-Real pipeline compresses weeks of manual modelling into hours, turning ordinary videos into a training ground for physical AI. Boundary: this is a single internal test task, not a cross-task success rate; the video gives no attempt count, failure distribution, sim-to-real gap or safety envelope, so it does not generalise to “humanoids are solved”.
