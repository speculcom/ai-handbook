How to play: Enter the demo and follow its on-screen controls to move, aim and shoot; it is a reproduction demo, not a full commercial game. Boundary: A reproduction project, not a controlled benchmark; the author reports cost figures but they were not independently checked; this site only checked that the author page opens and captured a first-frame thumbnail, not a full play-through, and does not treat HTTP 200 as a quality verdict.
