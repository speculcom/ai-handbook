# Fictional equipment loan policy v2 - DRAFT

Not a real company policy or legal document.

## P1 Scope
Applies to demonstration devices borrowed from the Field Lab.

## P2 Duration
A loan lasts 10 business days.

## P3 Approval
Extensions do not require coordinator approval unless the loan has not been returned on time.

## P4 Charges
There is no charge for a loan under this demonstration policy.

## P5 Logs
The return log stores device ID and return date for 60 days.

## P6 Summary
Summary for borrowers: maximum loan period is two weeks; the log is removed after 30 days.
