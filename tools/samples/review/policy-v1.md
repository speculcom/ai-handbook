# Fictional equipment loan policy v1

Not a real company policy or legal document.

## P1 Scope
Applies to demonstration devices borrowed from the Field Lab.

## P2 Duration
A loan lasts 14 calendar days.

## P3 Approval
The lab coordinator approves an extension before the original return date.

## P4 Charges
There is no charge for a loan under this demonstration policy.

## P5 Logs
The return log stores device ID and return date for 30 days.
